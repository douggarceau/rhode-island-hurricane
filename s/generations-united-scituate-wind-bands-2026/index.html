Youth and Adult Bands Share the Stage in North Scituate on Nov. 15

Friday, Nov. 15 at 7:15 p.m. at Scituate High School in North Scituate, where youth and adult wind bands share one concert.

A joint concert called Generations United brings two bands onto one stage at Scituate High School on Friday, Nov. 15, at 7:15 p.m. The program pairs the Youth Wind Ensembles of the RI Philharmonic Music School with the RI Wind Ensemble, an adult community band. The title points to the mix of ages on stage, with students and adult players sharing the same evening.

The young players

The Youth Wind Ensembles perform Gordon Jacob's William Byrd Suite, Frank Ticheli's Shenandoah and John Philip Sousa's Sabre and Spurs. Jacob's suite is built on music by William Byrd, the Renaissance English composer. Shenandoah is a traditional American folk song, and Ticheli's wind band setting puts it in the hands of the young players. Sabre and Spurs is a march by Sousa, the bandmaster best known for his marches.

The adult band

The RI Wind Ensemble is an adult community band, and its part of the program includes Beethoven's Egmont Overture and Four Dances from Leonard Bernstein's West Side Story. Beethoven wrote the Egmont music as the score for Goethe's play of the same name. The Bernstein dances come from the Broadway show, so the adult band moves from the concert repertoire to the musical stage in one set.

Together on stage

The two groups then join for a combined set: Henry Fillmore's The Klaxon and The Circus Bee, Samuel Hazo's Arabesque, and Eric Whitacre's Sleep. Whitacre is best known for his choral music, so his piece brings a different sound to a band program. Sharing the stage in this way gives the younger players a chance to play beside experienced adult musicians.

Tickets and location

Tickets are $12 for general admission and $7 for children, students and senior citizens. Tickets are sold at the door on the day of the concert, so there is no advance sale to arrange. Scituate High School is at 94 Trimtown Road in North Scituate.
