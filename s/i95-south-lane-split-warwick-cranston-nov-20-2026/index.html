I-95 South Lane Split Coming to Warwick-Cranston Line on Nov. 20

RIDOT will shift southbound traffic near the Jefferson Boulevard exit next Friday night so crews can rebuild bridges over the Pawtuxet River.

Drivers heading south on Interstate 95 through Cranston and Warwick will meet a new traffic pattern starting next Friday night, Nov. 20, when the Rhode Island Department of Transportation installs a lane split about a half mile before the Jefferson Boulevard off-ramp at Exit 31C, the agency announced Friday. The temporary layout is expected to stay in place until spring 2027.

What is changing

Under the new pattern, three lanes will run to the left of the split and one lane to the right, according to RIDOT. All four lanes continue on I-95 South. Drivers who want Exit 31C for Jefferson Boulevard must be in the single right-hand lane.

Exits 31A and 31B, the ramps to Route 37 East toward Warwick and Route 37 West toward Cranston, are not affected, the department said. Drivers can reach those exits from either the single right lane or the first lane to the left of the split.

RIDOT urged drivers not to stop or make sudden lane changes at the split itself, saying that behavior can cause delays and collisions.

Why the work is needed

The split lets crews replace, in phases, the bridges that carry I-95 over the Pawtuxet River and over Jefferson Boulevard, according to the release. Each traffic configuration opens a work zone where part of a bridge is demolished and rebuilt while traffic continues on the rest.

The work is part of RIDOT's I-95 15 Bridges project, which aims to clear 15 bridges along I-95 and Route 10 between Providence and Warwick from the state's list of bridges in poor or fair-to-poor condition. According to the department, nine of the 15 are structurally deficient, and three rank among the five most heavily traveled structurally deficient bridges in Rhode Island. At the Huntington Viaduct in Providence, the project includes a redesign of the entire Route 10 and I-95 interchange.

What it means for drivers

Drivers who regularly use the Jefferson Boulevard exit will need to be in the single right lane well before the ramp, while those heading to Route 37 will have two lanes to choose from.

RIDOT's notice was a reminder of the earlier announcement, giving drivers a week to plan. The department noted that all of its work is subject to change in schedule and scope based on conditions, findings and weather.

What comes next

The department expects to adjust the southbound split in spring 2027 and to add a second lane split for northbound traffic at that time. All lanes are expected to return to their original configuration by spring 2029, when the bridge replacements are scheduled to be complete.

RIDOT has posted an animation of the new lane split and additional project information on its website, at ridot.net under the I-95 15 project page. Separately, the department announced a Nov. 13 closure of the on-ramp from North Broadway to the Henderson Bridge in East Providence.
