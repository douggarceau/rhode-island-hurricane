Ballet RI's Nutcracker Returns to The VETS, Dec. 17-27

Yury Yanowsky's production of Tchaikovsky's holiday ballet plays seven days at the Veterans Memorial Auditorium in Providence.

Ballet Rhode Island, the Providence company formerly known as Festival Ballet, opens its 49th season with the holiday classic The Nutcracker at the Veterans Memorial Auditorium (The VETS), 1 Avenue of the Arts, Providence. Performances run December 17 through December 27, 2026.

The production

The staging is the work of the company's resident choreographer, Yury Yanowsky, set to Tchaikovsky's score. The ballet follows Clara from a Christmas Eve celebration into the Land of Sweets, with toy soldier battles and whirling snowflakes along the way. Professional company artists perform alongside young dancers from across Southern New England, a tradition that makes the production a rite of passage for local students.

The ballet has deep roots in the company itself. Its founders, Winthrop Corey and Christine Hennessy, created their own version of The Nutcracker nearly fifty years ago, in the company's first season, so the current production carries a long local lineage.

Dates and times

All times are 2026. Weeknight and evening performances: Thursday, December 17 and Friday, December 18 at 7:00 p.m.; Saturday, December 19 and Sunday, December 20 at 6:30 p.m.; Wednesday, December 23 at 6:30 p.m.; and Saturday, December 26 at 6:30 p.m. Matinees: December 19, 20 and 26 at 1:00 p.m., and Sunday, December 27 at 1:00 p.m. The matinees include an optional "Meet the Stars" experience after the performance, with a swag bag and a chance to meet the cast after the final bow.

Tickets

Tickets range from $36 to $130. The company says the only authorized sellers are thevetsri.com and, for groups of ten or more, ppacri.org. Tickets bought from other sellers may be marked up or invalid, so buy through those two sites.

With seven performance days stretched across the holiday week, it is among the easier ways to bring a family to the ballet.
