Nicky Jam Brings Perreoland to Amica the Saturday After Thanksgiving

Saturday, Nov. 28 at 8:00 PM at Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence, with tickets through the listing sites.

Nicky Jam is coming to Providence, and the Saturday after Thanksgiving is the night to be there. Perreoland featuring Nicky Jam is set for Amica Mutual Pavilion on Saturday, November 28, with the show starting at 8:00 PM. The ticket listings bill it as Perreoland: Nicky Jam, so the night is built around him.

The Perreoland name points to a dance-focused night, and Nicky Jam is one of reggaeton's best-known voices. If you want to be close to the stage, plan to arrive early, since a show this size fills the floor quickly.

Plan around it

Thanksgiving is Thursday, and the holiday weekend in Rhode Island is busy. The Warren Holiday Festival has events on November 27 and 28, including trolley tours, crafts and carolers. PPAC's 'Twas the Night Before... by Cirque du Soleil has Saturday shows at 1:00 PM, 4:00 PM and 7:30 PM. The Heritage Ballet's Nutcracker is at the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket at 2:00 PM the same day, and the Newport Nutcracker at Rosecliff is also on the calendar for November 28.

Door times and any opening acts were not listed in the sources we checked, so confirm the schedule with the venue before you go. Plan for holiday traffic and give yourself extra time to get to Amica Mutual Pavilion.

Tickets are sold through the listing sites, and prices change with seat and demand. Compare the options and check the venue's own page before you buy, so you know exactly which seat you are getting.

Whatever you pick, make it a full day. The holiday weekend has more on the calendar than usual, and a Saturday night show is a good anchor for everything else around it.
