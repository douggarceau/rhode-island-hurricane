The Fockers Crash Thanksgiving, Up Against Disney and Haymitch

Focker-In-Law opens Wednesday against Disney's Hexed and a second weekend of the new Hunger Games.

The matchup

Thanksgiving weekend at the movies comes down to a family that cannot stop embarrassing itself, a new Disney cartoon and a dystopian holdover. Universal's Focker-In-Law opens Wednesday, November 25, which gives it the full five-day holiday stretch, Slashfilm reported.

It is the fourth Meet the Parents movie, and the first since Little Fockers in 2010. John Hamburg directs. Ben Stiller, Robert De Niro, Blythe Danner and Owen Wilson are back. Ariana Grande joins as Olivia, alongside Skyler Gisondo. Slashfilm noted that Paramount is handling the release overseas.

The numbers

Slashfilm cited a forecast from Box Office Theory of as much as $56 million over the five days, and an over-under of $50 million for the debut. The outlet estimated the budget at around $100 million based on the cast, which is a guess, not a studio figure.

The franchise has history on its side. According to Slashfilm, Meet the Parents made $330 million worldwide in 2000 on a $55 million budget, Meet the Fockers topped $500 million, and Little Fockers made $310 million.

The competition

Disney's animated Hexed, with Hailee Steinfeld in the voice cast, is tracking for a softer opening in the $30 million range, per Slashfilm. And the holdover to beat is The Hunger Games: Sunrise on the Reaping. Slashfilm expected the prequel to open with at least $100 million domestically and to fight for the top spot again in its second weekend.

Why it matters

Studio comedies have mostly gone to streaming, and the ones that hit theaters this year have had a rough time. Slashfilm pointed to two 2026 comedies that flopped on the same weekend. A Focker-In-Law win would be a sign that audiences will still leave the couch to laugh in a room full of strangers, which is the whole argument for comedy on a big screen.

What comes next

Focker-In-Law hits theaters Wednesday. First numbers usually land Thursday morning, with the full five-day tally on Sunday.
