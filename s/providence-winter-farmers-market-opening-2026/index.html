Providence Winter Farmers Market Opens Its Season Saturday, Nov. 7

Saturday, Nov. 7 from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at 10 Sims Avenue in Providence, with local farm and food vendors under one roof.

The Providence winter farmers market opens its season on Saturday, Nov. 7, from 9:00 a.m. to 1:00 p.m. at 10 Sims Avenue in Providence. The market's listing shows the address as Unit 103 of the same building.

Under one roof

The market describes itself as the largest indoor farmers market in New England, and it runs from a custom-built facility at its headquarters. The winter season runs from November through April, and during those months 65 or more local farm and food vendors fill the building. The lineup changes monthly, so each visit can bring something new.

Paying for your basket

The market accepts cash, credit and debit cards, WIC, SNAP and EBT, and Bonus Bucks, so shoppers have several ways to pay. Pets are not allowed inside, following Health Department rules.

Why go on a Saturday morning

Saturday mornings are a pleasant time to browse before lunch. The market is open until 1:00 p.m., so a visit can leave room to try a few stands and choose something for the week ahead. Admission is no charge.
