A Community Evening With Cloth of the Earth at RISD Museum

A drop-in evening on Thursday, Nov. 12, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. at the RISD Museum in Providence, with gallery talks in Creole and English.

On Thursday, Nov. 12, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m., the RISD Museum opens its galleries for a community evening built around Cloth of the Earth: Indigo, Cape Verde, and West African Knowledge. The gathering costs nothing to attend and is presented by RISD's Social Equity and Inclusion Division, its Center for Community Partnerships and the museum.

Two gallery talks

The evening includes two gallery talks about the exhibition's histories, its technical elements and its cultural meaning. The first, from 6 to 6:30 p.m., is given in Creole. The second, from 6:45 to 7:15 p.m., is given in English. Maria Tavares, a collaborator on the exhibition, leads both talks, and Kate Irvin, the museum's Curator of Costume & Textiles, joins the English talk.

Drop in, and sign up for the talks

Visiting the galleries is drop-in, and light refreshments are served in the Grand Gallery. Each gallery talk has its own online registration form, so anyone who wants a seat in a particular talk should sign up through the museum's page first.

Why it is worth the evening

A talk given in Creole welcomes visitors to hear the exhibition in that language, which fits a show about textile knowledge that moves between places and communities. The English talk gives a second chance to hear the same material with the curator who leads the textile collection.

Getting there

The RISD Museum is at 20 North Main Street in Providence, and its phone number is 401-454-6500. Come early if you want a seat in one of the talks, since each one is separately registered.
