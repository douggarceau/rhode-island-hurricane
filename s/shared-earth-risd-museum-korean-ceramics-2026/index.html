Korean Ceramic Artists Demonstrate at RISD Museum on Nov. 11

Visiting artists show their ceramic processes from 10:30 a.m. to 4 p.m. in the Common Room, followed by a 6:30 p.m. panel at the RISD Museum in Providence.

On Wednesday, Nov. 11, the RISD Museum in Providence hosts visiting ceramic artists from Korea as part of its Shared Earth program, Contemporary Korean Artists Across Continents. The artists on the program are Audrey An, Heesoo Lee, Sunkoo Yuh, Min Soo Yuh and Kyungmin Park. The day is presented by RISD's Ceramic Department together with the museum.

Demonstrations in the Common Room

From 10:30 a.m. to 4 p.m., the artists demonstrate ceramic processes in the Common Room. Visitors can watch the work take shape and learn about the history of Korean ceramics. The museum's listing says the artists will show how tradition shapes ideas about material, form, surface, identity and process, and how they also explore new approaches. The demonstrations are drop-in, at no charge, and no registration is mentioned.

An evening panel

The day continues with a panel discussion from 6:30 to 8 p.m., which gives audiences a chance to hear more from the artists. The listing does not say whether the panel has a fee or needs a reservation, so check with the museum before you go. The panel has its own event page on the museum's site.

A work on the program

The event page features Audrey An's Barney Bani: Mirror Mirror (2022), a hand-built work in ceramic and mixed media. It is one of the pieces that shows how the artists combine clay with other materials. The work is shown alongside the demonstrations, so visitors can connect the finished pieces with the process.

Visiting

The RISD Museum is at 20 North Main Street in Providence, and its phone number is 401-454-6500. The event pages do not list exhibition run dates, so visitors who plan to see the galleries should confirm them with the museum.
