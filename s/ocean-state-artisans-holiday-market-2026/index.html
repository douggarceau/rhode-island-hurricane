Ocean State Artisans' Holiday Market Opens Thanksgiving Weekend in Wakefield

Friday and Saturday, Nov. 27-28, from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. at South Kingstown High School in Wakefield, with a food drive.

The Ocean State Artisans Holiday Market returns to South Kingstown High School in Wakefield for Thanksgiving weekend, open 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. on Friday, Nov. 27, and Saturday, Nov. 28. Ocean State Artisans is the organizer, and the show has run for more than 30 years, giving shoppers a chance to meet the makers in person.

What to expect

Every item for sale must be handcrafted, and the market is juried, so each applicant is reviewed before a booth is offered. The group has asked its vendors to join its membership. This year several categories are full, including jewelry, resin art, bath and body care, ocean-themed art, crochet and knitwear, and specialty food or pastry. Those categories can still request a place on a wait list, and the rest of the lineup is set by the jury.

A food drive alongside the shopping

The event is billed as a Holiday Market and Food Drive, a pairing that lets shoppers give back during the holiday weeks. The organizer's page does not list the drive's collection details, so anyone who wants to donate should check the group's Facebook page at facebook.com/OSArtisans for specifics before the show.

Plan your visit

The market runs both days, so visitors can browse on Friday and return on Saturday for another round. The organizer's page does not list an admission charge, so check with the group before you go. South Kingstown High School is in Wakefield.
