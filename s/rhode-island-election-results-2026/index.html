Rhode Island Results: Foulkes Elected Governor, Two Bonds Fail

Democrat Helena Foulkes wins the governor's race, and voters approve three of five statewide bond questions while rejecting two for the first time since 2006.

Rhode Island has a new governor-elect. Democrat Helena Foulkes won Tuesday's race, finishing ahead of independent Ken Block and Republican Aaron Guckian, according to unofficial results from the Rhode Island Board of Elections. Block conceded Tuesday night, NHPR reported.

The governor's race

The board's results show Foulkes with 57.5 percent of the vote, Block with 27.9 percent and Guckian with 13.2 percent. Foulkes is a former president of CVS Pharmacy.

The five bond questions

Voters approved three of the five statewide bond questions and rejected two. The defeats of Questions 3 and 4 are the first for statewide bond proposals since 2006, according to RI News Today and NBC 10.
