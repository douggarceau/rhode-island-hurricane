Rhode Island Results: Foulkes Elected Governor

Democrat Helena Foulkes wins the governor's race, and most of the five statewide bond questions appear headed for approval.

Rhode Island has a new governor-elect. Democrat Helena Foulkes won Tuesday's race, finishing ahead of independent Ken Block and Republican Aaron Guckian, according to unofficial results reported by NHPR and Ocean State Media. Block conceded Tuesday night.

The governor's race

Unofficial results reported by NHPR showed Foulkes with about 57 percent of the vote, Block with about 28 percent and Guckian with about 13 percent. Foulkes is a former president of CVS Pharmacy. Final, certified totals will come from the Rhode Island Board of Elections.

The five bond questions

Counts were still coming in overnight, and two of the five statewide questions were close, according to Ocean State Media and Yahoo News reporting.
