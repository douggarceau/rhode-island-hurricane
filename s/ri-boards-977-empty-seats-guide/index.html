977 Empty Seats: Inside Rhode Island's State Boards and Commissions

The state's 505 staffed boards and commissions, with 1,853 members listed past the end of their terms.

Rhode Island has hundreds of boards and commissions that license professions, set rates, approve spending and advise state agencies. The Secretary of State's database lists 616 of them, and 505 have members on record.

Across those boards, 977 seats are empty, and 1,853 members are listed past the end of their terms. Many members serve until they're replaced, and sometimes the state was never told about a new appointment, so a lapsed term doesn't always mean a lapsed seat.
