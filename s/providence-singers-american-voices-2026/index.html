Providence Singers Sing American Voices in Warren and Providence

Saturday, Nov. 21 at 7:00 p.m. at St. Mary of the Bay in Warren, and Sunday, Nov. 22 at 3:00 p.m. at Central Congregational Church in Providence.

The Providence Singers, the choral ensemble led by Artistic Director Christine Noel, opens its 2026–27 concert season with a program called American Voices. The concert comes in two performances in two different buildings. The first is Saturday, Nov. 21 at 7:00 p.m. at St. Mary of the Bay, 645 Main Street in Warren. The second is Sunday, Nov. 22 at 3:00 p.m. at Central Congregational Church, 296 Angell Street in Providence.

What the program holds

As the group describes the concert, it is a celebration of American music, built around beautiful arrangements of folk songs and the music of Aaron Copland. Copland's open harmonies and plainspoken melodies suit a chorus that likes clear, unhurried lines, and folk material gives singers a chance to shape a simple tune with real warmth.

The program also includes The World Beloved: A Bluegrass Mass, performed with the bluegrass band Gentlemen's Gambit. Pairing a sung Mass with a string band makes for an evening unlike most others on the fall calendar.

Why it is worth the trip

Choral concerts are some of the most welcoming evenings on the arts calendar. You do not need to know the repertoire, and a program that moves from Copland to folk tunes to bluegrass gives every kind of listener a place to land. Hearing the same program in Warren and in Providence also means hearing it in two different buildings, which is a pleasure in itself.

About the Providence Singers

The group's office is at 667 Waterman Avenue in East Providence. Its website describes concerts, recordings, commissioned music and streaming. The season continues with Handel's Messiah, performed with the Rhode Island Philharmonic Orchestra on Dec. 13 at 3:00 p.m. at The VETS, and with a Missa Solemnis with the Rhode Island Philharmonic on May 1, 2027, also at The VETS.
