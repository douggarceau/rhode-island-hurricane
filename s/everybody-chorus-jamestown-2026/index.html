Everybody Chorus Welcomes Jamestown Singers of All Ages

Saturday, Nov. 14 at 9:30 a.m. at the Jamestown Recreation Center, 41 Conanicus Avenue, with an informal singing hour open to all ages.

Everybody Chorus is an informal singing hour at the Jamestown Recreation Center, 41 Conanicus Avenue, open to all ages. The next session is Saturday, Nov. 14, at 9:30 a.m. Anyone interested should check with the director about any holiday changes to the schedule before coming.

The group is listed on the website of the Jamestown Community Chorus, a no-audition ensemble of about 50 singers. The chorus performs American music from every era, from early American songs to Show Boat, and its May concerts have included works by George M. Cohan, Phil Ochs and Rodgers and Hammerstein.

An informal singing hour asks little of a newcomer beyond showing up and joining in. It is a welcoming way to find out whether a choral group suits you before you commit to a season.

Community singing is one of the simplest pleasures of the season, and a Saturday morning at the rec center is a good place to start.

Details

Saturday, Nov. 14, 9:30 a.m., Jamestown Recreation Center, 41 Conanicus Avenue, Jamestown, RI. All ages are welcome.
