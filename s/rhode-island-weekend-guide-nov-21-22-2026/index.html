Weekend Guide: Noise Rock, Mansion Lights and Frozen, Nov. 21-22

A Pawtucket punk festival, the first Sparkling Lights night at The Breakers and a 150-vendor holiday market lead a busy Rhode Island weekend.

The weekend before Thanksgiving is when Rhode Island flips into holiday mode, and this one does it loudly. A punk and noise festival takes over The Met in Pawtucket on Saturday, The Breakers in Newport opens its Sparkling Lights evenings, Woonsocket's Stadium Theatre stages Frozen and more than 150 vendors pack the West Warwick Civic Center. Here is how to fill Saturday, Nov. 21, and Sunday, Nov. 22.

Saturday night: get loud in Pawtucket

The headline for anyone who likes guitars is Kafkaesque Providence 2026 at The Met, 1005 Main St. in Pawtucket. Songkick lists King Missile, Dropdead, Sunburned Hand of the Man, Weird Nightmare, PONS, Flesh Narc, Drunks With Guns and Baylies Band on the bill, a lineup that runs from art-punk to hardcore to experimental noise. The listings did not show times or prices, so check themetri.com. The Met is back on Sunday with The Wankers, whom Songkick files under British Invasion.

Newport after dark

Sparkling Lights at The Breakers opens Saturday, Nov. 21, and runs again Sunday, according to the Preservation Society of Newport County. Every guest needs a timed ticket, with entries every half hour from 4 to 6 p.m., and the house and grounds close at 8 p.m. The timed ticket includes the mansion's interior. The society recommends buying ahead; day-of tickets are sold only if the night has not sold out. The evenings continue on selected dates through Dec. 27.

Shop the season in West Warwick

The Rhode Island Christmas Festival brings more than 150 local vendors, entertainment and food trucks to the West Warwick Civic Center at 100 Factory St., according to Visit Rhode Island. Hours are 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. Saturday and 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. Sunday. Admission and parking carry no charge. The listing shows slightly different hours in one section, so confirm Sunday times with the organizers.

Big stages

In Woonsocket, the Stadium Theatre on Monument Square wraps up its weekend of Disney's Frozen: The Broadway Musical, with performances Saturday at 2 and 7:30 p.m. and Sunday at 2 p.m. The theatre's event page listed tickets at $29, $36 and $41. In Providence, Trinity Repertory Company's A Christmas Carol is in the middle of its 50th-anniversary run at the Dowling Theater, directed by Taavon Gamble and playing through Dec. 31.

Plan the weekend

Pawtucket, West Warwick, Woonsocket and Newport are all within easy reach of Providence, so a full Saturday is doable: shop the West Warwick market in the morning, catch the Frozen matinee or a Breakers timed entry in the late afternoon, and finish at The Met. Sunday works as the slower day, with the market's shorter hours, the final Frozen matinee and an evening show at The Met.

Before you go

Times and prices change, especially at club shows and holiday events, so check each venue's website on the day. The Preservation Society recommends buying Sparkling Lights tickets in advance.
