Trinity Rep's A Christmas Carol Celebrates Its 50th Anniversary

Trinity Repertory Company's 50th-anniversary A Christmas Carol runs through Dec. 31 at the Dowling Theater in Providence, directed by Taavon Gamble.

Trinity Repertory Company in Providence is marking the 50th anniversary of its A Christmas Carol this season, and the production is running through December 31 at the Dowling Theater. Taavon Gamble directs the staging of Charles Dickens's story of Ebenezer Scrooge and the spirits who visit him on Christmas Eve. The show opened November 5.

A holiday tradition with a milestone

Few seasonal productions in Rhode Island have the staying power of this one. A 50th-anniversary staging gives longtime audiences a chance to return to a familiar tradition and gives new visitors a polished introduction to a story that has been retold on stages for generations. The company lists its performances and ticket options on its website, so check there before you plan a family outing.

Where to see it

Performances take place at the Dowling Theater in Providence while the company's Chace Theater is under renovation. Single tickets and performance times are listed at trinityrep.com under the 2026-27 season page. Because the run ends in late December, the production is a good choice for a weekend in the next few weeks.

What comes next at Trinity Rep

The company's 2026-27 season continues in the new year with Cyrano, which runs from January 27 through March 18, 2027, and Mother Russia, from February 11 through March 21, in rotation with Cyrano. August Wilson's Ma Rainey's Black Bottom follows from April 8 to May 9, and Nothing Up My Sleeve, a magic show by Dendy directed by Aaron Posner, closes the season from May 21 to June 20, 2027.
