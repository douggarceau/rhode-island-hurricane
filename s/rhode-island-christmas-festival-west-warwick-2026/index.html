Rhode Island Christmas Festival Brings 150 Vendors to West Warwick

Saturday, Nov. 21, 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., at the West Warwick Civic Center in West Warwick, with Sunday hours on Nov. 22.

The Rhode Island Christmas Festival opens the holiday shopping season at the West Warwick Civic Center, 100 Factory Street, on Nov. 21 and 22. More than 150 local vendors fill the hall, and the event describes itself as a family-friendly holiday gathering with entertainment and food trucks.

What You'll Find

Exhibitors sell food and treats, crafts and handmade gifts, apparel and jewelry, bath and health products, and home and garden items. A few specialty services are also on hand. The event page mentions entertainment without naming any performers, so the afternoon is best treated as a browse through a busy marketplace alongside food and entertainment.

Hours and Parking

Saturday runs from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. The event listing gives Sunday, Nov. 22, as 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. in its hours section, although its summary shows 10 a.m. to 4 p.m. for both days, so visitors planning a Sunday trip should check the organizers' posts before heading out. Admission and parking both carry no charge.

A Good Fit for Families

Because admission costs nothing and food trucks are on site, the festival suits a short outing with children or a relaxed afternoon of gift shopping before the Thanksgiving holiday. It is a practical way to start the season without committing to a full day.
