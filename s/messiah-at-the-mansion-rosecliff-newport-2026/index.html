Handel's Messiah Comes to Rosecliff Mansion in Newport on Dec. 6

Sunday, Dec. 6 at 1 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. at Rosecliff Mansion, 548 Bellevue Avenue, Newport; doors open at 12:30 p.m.

Handel's Messiah, first heard in Dublin in 1742, is one of the most loved works in the choral repertory, and on Sunday, Dec. 6 Newport Classical brings a chamber version of it to Rosecliff Mansion on Bellevue Avenue. The concert runs about an hour with no intermission, and it is offered at 1 p.m. and again at 3:30 p.m.

The performance is shared by Ensemble Altera, a Rhode Island vocal ensemble led by countertenor Christopher Lowrey, and the Professional Choristers of The Choir School of Newport County. Together they present a reimagined version that Newport Classical describes as a bold, fresh take on a familiar work, one that suits longtime listeners and first-time audiences alike.

Why hear it in a mansion

Rosecliff is a Gilded Age summer cottage built on Bellevue Avenue at the turn of the twentieth century, and its ballroom and salons give the voices a warm, intimate setting. Because the program is shorter than a full oratorio, it works well for families, for guests who have never attended a Messiah, and for anyone planning a December afternoon in Newport. The program closes with the Hallelujah Chorus.

Details

Doors open at 12:30 p.m. Tickets are $75 for premier seating and $60 for main seating. Purchase through newportclassical.org, where each performance has its own event page. Rosecliff Mansion is at 548 Bellevue Avenue, Newport, RI 02840.
