Ringing In 2027: Rhode Island's New Year's Eve Guide

Noon countdowns, Providence's riverfront fireworks, tasting dinners, black-tie balls, live bands and comedy across the state on Thursday, Dec. 31.

Rhode Island has plenty of ways to say goodbye to 2026, from noon countdowns at libraries and a skating rink to Providence's riverfront fireworks and black-tie balls on the Watch Hill shore. Here is what organizers have posted so far for Thursday, Dec. 31, and New Year's Day, from family afternoons and tasting menus to live bands, comedy and midnight toasts.

All times are Eastern. Prices are as listed by each organizer and may not include fees, tax or gratuity. Many details are still being posted, so check with the venue before you go.

Providence's New Year's Eve celebration

Providence's citywide celebration runs 5 to 9 p.m. at 195 District Park and the Michael Van Leesten Memorial Pedestrian Bridge. It's open to all ages at no charge, with food, entertainment, interactive installations and fireworks at 8:30 p.m., according to the Providence Tourism Council.

Throughout the evening, a mainstage DJ, pop-up dance performances and roaming performers fill the park; their set times weren't posted. Families can try the after-dark illuminated playground with glowing games and swings, plus face painting and balloon twisting at no charge. There's also a 360-degree photo booth, an enchanted forest with New Year's wishing trees, and inflatable sculptures and photo displays.

WaterFire calls its part of the night Starts at Sunset. Its event page lists 5 to 9 p.m. at the Michael S. Van Leesten Memorial Bridge, 365 S. Water St., where volunteers light five braziers for a community gathering, with the lighting and torch procession at 5:30 p.m.

For families

United Skates of America in Rumford holds its Noon New Year's Eve Party with the Trolls from 10 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., a family celebration with skating and optional climbing or laser tag, according to Rhode Island Moms. Confirm this year's event directly with the rink.

Dudek Lanes in Warren runs Rock 'n' Bowl with two-hour reservations through the afternoon and evening. Bowling, shoes, lights and music are included, with food for purchase. Call the lanes to confirm hours and book a lane.

Libraries and museums hold noon countdowns for younger children. The Warwick Public Library's Children's Library hosts Noon Year's Eve from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. with a dress-up theme, crafts, music, snacks and a noon countdown. In South Kingstown, the Kingston library's Kids Noon Year's Eve runs noon to 2 p.m. in Potter Hall, with crafts, games, open play and a noon balloon drop; the library says no food will be served.

Rhode Island Moms also lists MobileQuest's Countdown to Noon Year in Cranston, 10 a.m. to noon, with STEM activities for ages 4 to 12 with an adult; the Providence Children's Museum, 9:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m., with performances, paper crowns and a confetti countdown, though the museum's page still showed last year; and noon programs at Cross' Mills Library in Charlestown and the public library in North Providence (11 a.m. to noon, registration required). Confirm these with each venue.

In Charlestown, a New Year's Eve bonfire at Ninigret Park, 5 Park Lane, has been listed for 4:30 to 7 p.m., but the only source we found is a December 2025 announcement, so the 2026 time and date are unconfirmed. In Warwick, a past Rocky Point listing from the Central Rhode Island Chamber describes food trucks and festivities from 3 p.m. and fireworks at 6 p.m., but the 2026 event isn't confirmed, so check with the city before you go.

Three light displays stay open on New Year's Eve. Roger Williams Park Zoo's Holiday Lights Spectacular, 1000 Elmwood Ave., runs 5 to 9:30 p.m. on Dec. 31 and Jan. 1, with more than 3.5 million lights across 40 acres; advance online tickets are $13 to $15. The Rhode Island Lights Festival at Centreville Bank Stadium, 11 Tidewater St., Pawtucket, runs 5 to 10 p.m. nightly through Jan. 3, except Dec. 24 and 25. In Warwick, the Lewis Family Christmas Light Show, 12 Shenandoah Road, runs its last night from 5:30 to 8:55 p.m., at no charge; tune to 106.9 FM for the music.

On stage, in church and in quiet practice

Trinity Rep's 50th-anniversary production of A Christmas Carol runs through Dec. 31 at the Dowling Theater, 201 Washington St., Providence. The New Year's Eve performance time was not listed on the calendar we checked.

Newport Playhouse lists a Dec. 31 performance of Social Security, a comedy about a family visit that upends a couple's quiet life. The showtime and price aren't posted, and the theater says it will close on Dec. 31, 2026, so confirm availability directly.

St. Patrick Church, 244 Smith St., Providence, celebrates a New Year's Eve Mass at 6 p.m., interpreted in ASL, English and Spanish, according to the Diocese of Providence.

St. Michael the Archangel Church in Providence listed a bilingual New Year's Eve Mass at 6 p.m. in its December 2025 bulletin; confirm the 2026 time with the parish. In Warwick, St. Gregory the Great's calendar lists Mass at 8:30 a.m. on Dec. 31 at 360 Cowesett Road.

The Providence Zen Center, 99 Pound Road, Cumberland, posts a New Year's Eve practice and retreat with a community dinner, evening practice, midnight practice and an overnight stay for participants. Registration covers several days starting Dec. 30, so confirm the Dec. 31 schedule and availability.

Dinners to ring it in

Gracie's, 194 Washington St., Providence, lists An Evening in Bloom at 5 p.m., with a choice of a five- or seven-course seasonal tasting menu and optional wine pairings.

Claudine in Providence offers two seatings. The early dinner at 5 p.m. is a five-course chef's tasting at $155 per person before beverages, tax and gratuity, with seating ending at 7:30 p.m. The late dinner opens with an 8 p.m. welcome and an 8:30 p.m. ten-course tasting, then a midnight countdown and toast, at $350 per person before additional beverages, tax and gratuity.

The Weekapaug Inn, 25 Spray Rock Road, Westerly, serves a five-course dinner at 6 p.m. and again at 9 p.m., with live music in the Sea Room and an optional wine pairing.

Galas, balls and big parties

Ocean House in Watch Hill hosts two evenings. The Big Ben Black Tie Bash runs 8 p.m. to 1 a.m. for guests 21 and older at $550, with a Bistro reception, live entertainment and food stations, plus a DJ in the ballroom. The London Calling New Year's Eve Ball runs 9 p.m. to 1 a.m. in the Seaside Ballroom with a 1960s British theme, DJ, dancing, food stations, a photo set and costume prizes. Tickets are $275 for guests 21 and older and $175 for those under 21.

The Chanler, 117 Memorial Blvd., Newport, presents The Scarlet House from 7 p.m. to 1 a.m. at $385 plus service charge and tax. The crimson-themed night includes a reception, live modern violin, a four-course dinner, a DJ, dancing, late-night bites and a midnight toast.

Newport Harbor Island Resort on Goat Island has posted an 8:30 p.m. to 1 a.m. celebration. Entertainment and menu details were not yet on its event page.

Bassett House, 18 Bassett St., Providence, announces Velvet Midnight with live music, a DJ, chef-curated stations, passed hors d'oeuvres, dessert, late-night sliders and a toast. Ticket details and times were still pending.

Bally's Lincoln opens its Event Center at 8 p.m. for a 21+ party with a buffet, comedy from Steve Sweeney, the World Premier Band, aerial and disco performers, a DJ and the New York City ball drop on screen.

Live music, comedy and dancing

In Woonsocket, Chan's Egg Rolls & Jazz, 267 Main St., welcomes Crosswind, a Chris Stapleton tribute. Doors open at 7:30 p.m. and the show runs 9 p.m. to midnight, with a dance floor, hats, noisemakers and a midnight toast. Admission is $50, and an optional buffet is $30 plus tax and tip.

Moonshine Alley, 52 Pine St., Providence, has Nothing Personal playing from 9:30 p.m. to 12:30 a.m. Check with the venue for admission and tables.

On Jamestown, the Narragansett Cafe has the Down City Band playing New Year's Eve from 8:30 to 11:59 p.m. Price and age limits aren't posted.

The Dancing Feeling's ballroom New Year's Eve starts at 7 p.m. at Rhodes on the Pawtuxet, 60 Rhodes Place, Cranston, with hors d'oeuvres, a buffet, continuous dancing, a professional American Rhythm dance show, a midnight toast and dancing until 12:30 a.m.

Next Stop Comedy comes to Newport Craft Brewing & Distilling, 293 JT Connell Highway. Doors and seating open at 7:30 p.m. and the show starts at 8 p.m., with Giancarlo Biondino headlining and Mark McGwin hosting.

Little Miss and The Boom bring swing, blues, jazz and rockabilly to The Blue Room, 2200 Broad St., Cranston, in a 7:30 p.m. listing. Ticket and dinner details were still marked as coming soon.

Already sold out

Three Newport parties were marked sold out on the organizer's overview when we checked: Gas Lamp Newport, with 9:30 p.m. doors, DJ D-Wun and a countdown; Forty 1 North; and Midtown Newport, with a live band and DJ. All three are 21+.

New Year's Day: Jan. 1

A Wish Come True's 23rd Annual Polar Plunge Challenge, Freezin' for a Reason, starts at noon sharp at Easton's Beach, 175 Memorial Blvd., Newport, with an event window of noon to 1:30 p.m. Participation starts with a $25 donation, and spectators are welcome.

MENTOR Rhode Island's Frozen Clam Dip and Obsta-Plunge runs 11 a.m. to 1 p.m. at Goddard State Park Beach, 1095 Ives Road, Warwick, with a ninja-style obstacle course, a plunge, or both. Tickets are through the organizer; the page doesn't list a price.

Rhode Island State Parks has held a First Day Hike at Goddard Memorial State Park, a 1.5-mile loop from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m., but its page doesn't confirm a 2027 hike, so check before you go. St. Michael the Archangel Church in Providence listed a bilingual New Year's Day Mass at noon in its December 2025 bulletin; confirm the 2027 time with the parish. The zoo's Holiday Lights Spectacular and the Rhode Island Lights Festival are also open on New Year's Day.
