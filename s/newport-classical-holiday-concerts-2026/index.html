Newport Classical's Holiday Concerts Bring Choral Music to December

Christmas with Cantus, Messiah at the Mansion and Classical Christmas with Frisson on Dec. 5, 6 and 12, with early-afternoon showtimes in Newport.

Newport Classical runs two linked seasons: a twelve-concert chamber series that starts in the fall, and a summer festival that filled eleven venues with thirty concerts from July 2 to 19 this year. Its holiday programs add a seasonal layer to that calendar, and three December concerts are now on the schedule.

The chamber series opening

The 2026-27 chamber season opened on Sept. 18 at the Newport Classical Recital Hall, 42 Dearborn St., Newport. The quartet Invoke performed American Voices, a program built around the music of William Grant Still, Florence Price and Duke Ellington.

The December concerts

Christmas with Cantus is set for Saturday, Dec. 5, at 1 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. It is a vocal program and the first of the three holiday concerts.

Messiah at the Mansion follows on Sunday, Dec. 6, at 1 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. at Rosecliff, 548 Bellevue Ave., Newport, performed by a mixed ensemble.

Classical Christmas with Frisson closes the group on Saturday, Dec. 12, with showtimes at 1 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. Like the Messiah program, it is performed by a mixed ensemble.

Planning a visit

Rosecliff hosts Messiah at the Mansion, and the listing does not name a venue for the other two December concerts, so check newportclassical.org for locations, seating and tickets before you go. An afternoon showtime makes these concerts an easy outing for families and first-time listeners.
