State Ballet of Rhode Island's Coppélia Comes to Roberts Hall Dec. 12

Saturday, Dec. 12 at 2 p.m. in Roberts Hall Auditorium at Rhode Island College, Providence, with a pay-what-you-can dress rehearsal on Dec. 11.

The State Ballet of Rhode Island has presented its holiday production of Coppélia in the state since 1962. This year's staging comes to Roberts Hall Auditorium at Rhode Island College on Saturday, Dec. 12, at 2 p.m. The company describes it as a single performance, so families planning around the holidays should book early.

The story

Coppélia follows a comic tale of love and mischief centered on an eccentric toymaker and a set of life-sized dancing dolls. The company calls it a classical family production with bright, colorful scenery and costumes, and it recommends the ballet for children ages 5 and up.

Meet the dancers before the curtain

For those who want a closer look, a dress rehearsal is set for Friday, Dec. 11, at 10 a.m. in the same hall. Admission is pay what you can. The session walks through the ballet's story, its music, costumes and scenery, and gives guests a chance to meet the dancers. It is a useful preview for first-time viewers, especially younger ones.

Tickets and parking

Tickets for the performance are $40 per person, with all fees included. Parking is available at no charge, so the afternoon can be planned around a single trip to campus.
