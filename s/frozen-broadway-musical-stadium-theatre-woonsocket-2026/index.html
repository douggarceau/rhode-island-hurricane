Frozen Thaws Woonsocket's Stadium Theatre With a Broadway Musical

Four performances from Friday, Nov. 20 at 7:30 p.m. through Sunday, Nov. 22 at 2 p.m. at the Stadium Theatre, 28 Monument Square, Woonsocket.

Woonsocket's Stadium Theatre is bringing Disney's Frozen: The Broadway Musical to its stage for four performances over a single weekend. For families looking for a holiday-season outing, it is a chance to see a full stage production close to home.

The Story

The show adapts the Disney film about the sisters Anna and Elsa. When Elsa's magic plunges the kingdom into a deep winter, Anna sets out to bring her sister home, joined by the mountain guide Kristoff, the reindeer Sven and the snowman Olaf. Audiences who know the movie will hear its familiar songs, along with new music written for the stage.

Showtimes and Tickets

Performances run Friday, Nov. 20 at 7:30 p.m., Saturday, Nov. 21 at 2 p.m. and 7:30 p.m., and Sunday, Nov. 22 at 2 p.m. Tickets are $29, $36 and $41. The theatre lists the production as family friendly, and the box office is at the theatre on Monument Square.

Before You Go

The event page reviewed for this story did not list a running time, so families with younger children may want to call the box office ahead of the day. Seats at the two Saturday performances and the Sunday matinee are the most likely to suit a daytime outing, and the theatre's own calendar is the best place to confirm any changes to the schedule.
