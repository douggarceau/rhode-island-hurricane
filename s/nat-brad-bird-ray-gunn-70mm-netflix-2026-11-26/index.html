Brad Bird's Ray Gunn Goes Noir in 70mm Before It Hits Netflix

The Incredibles director's animated space-age detective story, with Sam Rockwell, Scarlett Johansson and Tom Waits, opens December 4.

The film

Brad Bird, the director behind The Incredibles and Ratatouille, has a new animated feature, and it is a detective noir. Ray Gunn opens in select theaters and in limited 70mm on December 4, then streams on Netflix on December 18, according to Netflix's Tudum site, which posted the first trailer this month.

Who's in it

Sam Rockwell voices Raymond Gunn, a private eye. Scarlett Johansson plays Venus Nova, a multimedia megastar who pulls him into a case. Tom Waits voices Eye-Ra, Ray's one-eyed alien partner, Netflix said. The film is rated PG.

The setup

The story takes place in Metropia, a retrofuturist city of flying cars and mile-high towers where humans and aliens live side by side. Ray is one of the last investigators who still works cases the old way, while robots handle most of the legwork, according to Tudum. Once Venus enters the picture, he follows a trail through her crowd of hangers-on, bouncing between the city's glossy heights and its grimy underworld. Murder and corruption come with the territory.

A long time coming

Bird first had the idea at the start of his career, Netflix said, and he has described it as a mashup of hard-boiled detective film and space-age adventure. His own shorthand, quoted by Tudum, is The Maltese Falcon meets Buck Rogers. The film nods to Bogart-era private eye pictures, the noir classic Out of the Past, and the world of Philip Marlowe and Sam Spade.

Why it is worth a look

Big-studio animation has spent years chasing family comedies and sequels. A noir about a burned-out gumshoe, from the director who made superhero families and a cooking rat feel like prestige cinema, is a different swing. The 70mm run is the tell: Netflix is treating this as a film you should see on a very large screen before it lands on your couch. Theater locations for the limited run have not been listed on the Tudum page, so check local listings as December 4 gets closer.
