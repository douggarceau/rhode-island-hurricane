Leftovers Are Done. Go Out: Rhode Island's Post-Thanksgiving Weekend

A boat parade tonight, Santa Magic on Long Wharf Saturday, the Providence Flea's holiday market Sunday and Rough Point after dark.

Thanksgiving is over and the state flips straight into holiday mode. Between tonight and Sunday, Newport and Providence both have plenty going on, from lit-up boats in the harbor to a vintage and makers' market in a Providence food hall, according to Discover Newport's holiday guide and event listings.

Tonight: boats on the water

The weekend starts on Newport Harbor. Discover Newport lists the Newport Harbor Illuminated Boat Parade for tonight, Friday, Nov. 27, at 6 p.m., with decorated boats competing for best decorated. It costs nothing to watch from the waterfront. Bundle up, find a spot along the harbor and let the boats come to you.

Saturday: Santa Magic and a Gilded Age house after dark

On Saturday, Nov. 28, the Shops at Long Wharf in Newport host Santa Magic from 4 to 6 p.m., per Discover Newport, with a tree lighting, photos with Santa, crafts for kids and music. There is no charge, and the guide lists Sunday, Nov. 29, as the rain date.

Up on Bellevue Avenue, A Rough Point Holiday is underway at Rough Point, 680 Bellevue Ave., running Nov. 27 through Dec. 27, from 10 a.m. to 4 p.m., according to the guide. General admission is $25 and students pay $12; children and Newport County residents get in at no charge. The guide also lists "After Dark" visits on Friday and Saturday evenings from 4 to 5:30 p.m.

Saturday also kicks off Coastal Queen Cruises' Holiday Cruise with the Very Merry Dickens Carolers, a 75-minute cruise on select dates through Dec. 20, per Discover Newport. And the guide's Holiday Lantern Walking Tour leaves at 4 p.m. Friday through Sunday through Dec. 27, taking walkers through Newport's historic streets.

Sunday: the Providence Flea goes holiday

Back in the capital, the 2026 Providence Holiday Flea Market fills Farm Fresh Market Hall at 10 Sims Ave. on Sunday, Nov. 29, from 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., according to its Fairs and Festivals listing. The listing promises local vintage vendors, artists, artisans and makers, bakeshops and community nonprofits, plus live musicians, hot coffee, craft beer and cocktails and food trucks. Admission and parking carry no charge, per the listing.

It is a good place to knock out gift shopping without a mall in sight, and the music makes it more of a hang than an errand.

Next weekend

Plan ahead for Saturday and Sunday, Dec. 5 and 6, when The Intentional Celebration: Holiday Edition takes over WaterFire Providence's building in Providence, according to Visit Rhode Island. The listing calls it a two-day indoor holiday market and immersive Christmas experience, with festive installations, hands-on workshops and live demonstrations on decorating, tablescapes, floral design and entertaining. General admission runs noon to 5 p.m. both days at no charge, with a paid VIP hour from 11 a.m. to noon.

Before you go

Times and prices come from each organizer's listing as published. Weather can move outdoor events, so check the Discover Newport guide and each organizer's page before you head out.
