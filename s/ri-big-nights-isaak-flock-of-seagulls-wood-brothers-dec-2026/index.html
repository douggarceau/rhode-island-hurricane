Chris Isaak, A Flock of Seagulls and More: Next Week's Big Rhode Island Nights

Cranston, East Greenwich and Westerly carry a loaded Dec. 2-5 run, with no trip to Providence needed.

Forget the idea that big shows only happen in Providence. Next week, the best nights out in Rhode Island run through Cranston, East Greenwich, Westerly and the South County shore, with Chris Isaak, A Flock of Seagulls, the Wood Brothers and Martin Sexton all on the calendar between Wednesday, Dec. 2, and Saturday, Dec. 5, according to JamBase's statewide concert listings.

Cranston's Park Theatre: three big nights in a row

The Park Theatre in Cranston has the most stacked week of any room in the state. JamBase lists Sara Evans on Wednesday, Dec. 2, Chris Isaak on Thursday, Dec. 3, and the Wood Brothers on Friday, Dec. 4. That is three very different crowds in three straight nights in the same room.

East Greenwich: new wave, a mashup and a voice

The Greenwich Odeum on Main Street in East Greenwich, a room Songkick lists with a capacity of about 500, has its own three-night run. A Flock of Seagulls play Thursday, Dec. 3, per both JamBase and Songkick, bringing a name from the new wave era to a room of about 500 seats.

Friday, Dec. 4, belongs to Pink Talking Fish. Then Saturday, Dec. 5, brings Martin Sexton, according to both listing sites.

And if you want to plan further out, Songkick shows Pure Prairie League at the Odeum on Friday, Dec. 11, with the Lemonheads due on Sunday, Feb. 7, 2027.

Westerly and the shore

Down in Westerly, the United Theatre hosts Sara Watkins on Friday, Dec. 4, with Watkins Family Hour on the bill, per JamBase. The same night, Everett Bradley plays Ocean Mist in South Kingstown.

Why it matters

For years the knock on Rhode Island was that the big tours stopped in Boston and skipped us. This week's lineup argues otherwise, and it does it in theaters outside the capital: the Park in Cranston, the Odeum in East Greenwich and the United in Westerly. Smaller rooms mean you can actually see the stage and still make it home on a work night.

How to go

Listing sites do not give full showtimes or prices for every show, so check each venue's own box office before you go. The Odeum is at 59 Main St. in East Greenwich, according to Songkick. With holiday parties filling up December weekends, buy early for the Friday shows, when four Rhode Island rooms have something going at once. If you live south of the Providence area, the United Theatre in Westerly and Ocean Mist in South Kingstown put Friday's music close to home, and both are on JamBase's list for the same night.

One more tip: the Thursday double, Chris Isaak in Cranston or A Flock of Seagulls in East Greenwich, is the toughest call of the week. Both start the same night, so you will have to pick one.
