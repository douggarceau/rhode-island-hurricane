Greek Food Fair and Christmas Bazaar Opens Sunday in Cranston

Sunday, Nov. 8 from 11 a.m. at the Church of the Annunciation, 175 Oaklawn Avenue, Cranston, with admission at no charge.

The Greek Food Fair and Christmas Bazaar opens at 11 a.m. Sunday at the Church of the Annunciation, a Greek Orthodox parish at 175 Oaklawn Avenue in Cranston. Admission is no charge, and the fair pairs Greek food with holiday shopping in one busy afternoon.

The fair is run by the parish's Ladies Philoptochos Society, whose name translates roughly as friends of the poor. In past years the bazaar has offered a pastry booth, a jewelry booth, handcrafts, vintage treasures, a white elephant sale, a silent auction and the Agora Greek Marketplace, along with raffles.

The proceeds go to charities. A report on an earlier fair named several beneficiaries, among them the Special Olympics, McAuley House, Ronald McDonald House and Hasbro. The society has run the fair for decades, and each year's bazaar adds to that record of giving.

Details

Sunday, Nov. 8, from 11 a.m., Church of the Annunciation, 175 Oaklawn Avenue, Cranston, RI. Admission is no charge. It is a good chance to buy holiday gifts and support local charities at the same time.
