Jamestown Community Theatre Opens Merrily We Roll Along

Performances run Nov. 19 through 22 in Jamestown, with a Sondheim musical that unfolds in reverse.

Jamestown Community Theatre presents Stephen Sondheim's Merrily We Roll Along, with performances running Nov. 19 through 22. The musical follows composer Franklin Shepard, who finds professional success at a steep personal cost.

A story told backward

The show unfolds in reverse. It begins where Frank and his friends are young and hopeful, then moves back toward the beginning of their story, so the audience watches the friendships and choices unravel in the opposite order from the way they happened.

Behind the scenes

Auditions were held on Thursday, Sept. 3, at the Jamestown Recreation Center. Rehearsals were set for Sundays from 1 to 5 p.m. and for Tuesdays and Thursdays from 6 to 9 p.m., with choreography intensives on two Fridays in October.

Plan your visit

Performance times and ticket prices were not in the announcement we read. Check the company's listing for showtimes and tickets. The production is a chance to see local performers on the island take on one of the most ambitious scores in the modern musical theatre.
