15,880 State Workers: What the Payroll Shows

Every agency's workforce, job titles and what each job involves, from the state's own payroll file.

Rhode Island publishes a file of everyone on the state payroll. The latest version, which the state last updated on Oct. 9, 2025, lists 15,880 people across 43 agencies and more than 2,100 job titles.

Rhode Island Hurricane's government guide turns that file into something you can read.

Free for everyone

Each agency shows how many people it has on the payroll and every job title it uses. For 151 of the most common titles, the guide explains what the job involves, from the state's official job descriptions.

For members

Members see every employee by name within each title, with pay rate, fiscal 2025 pay and overtime, along with each agency's turnover, raises and retirement wave, and a sortable roster they can download.

The payroll file is a dated snapshot: people hired or gone since October 2025 aren't in it.
