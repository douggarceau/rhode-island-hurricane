The Newport Nutcracker Returns to Rosecliff Mansion for Late November

Newport Contemporary Ballet dances its ballroom Nutcracker on Nov. 25, 27 and 28 at Rosecliff in Newport.

Few holiday productions are as tied to their setting as The Newport Nutcracker at Rosecliff. Newport Contemporary Ballet stages the work in the mansion's ballroom, where the party scene is built around the room itself. Artistic Director Danielle Genest has said the production was made for that space.

The Nutcracker marked its 25th anniversary in 2025, and the company reconceived its second act with a more distinctly Newport identity. Dance Informa, reviewing the November 2025 opening night, noted new variations and costumes by Deanna Gerde, with lighting by Stephen Petrilli. The changes keep the production rooted in the mansion itself, which is why the setting remains central to the performance.

The dancers

Tyler Diggs dances Drosselmeyer and also the Harbor Regatta. Eva Gail Mulrooney plays Tess Oelrichs, the production's Clara, while Abigail Molina dances the Ballerina Doll and Mia Nolte leads the Rat Queen. Margot Aknin and Josh Thake take the Snow Queen and King, and Lauren Difede is the Rose Fairy, partnered by Nathan Crewe-Kluge in the Grand Pas de Deux. Jenna Torgeson performs the Dew Drop role, reconceived as a hydrangea-inspired variation.

Dates and tickets

The production is scheduled for Wednesday, Nov. 25, at 5:30 p.m., and again on Friday, Nov. 27, and Saturday, Nov. 28, at Rosecliff Mansion on Bellevue Avenue in Newport. Check the company's website for showtimes on the later dates, prices and tickets.

It is a rare chance to see a ballet staged in a Gilded Age ballroom, with the audience seated in the same kind of rooms the party scene is meant to fill.
