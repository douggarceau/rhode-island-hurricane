Turnover, Thursday and Gene Simmons: Rhode Island Rock Is Booked Into 2027

Fete Music Hall and Bally's Event Center in Lincoln are stacking loud shows from Dec. 1 into the spring.

Rhode Island's rock calendar is already reaching into 2027. Fete Music Hall in Olneyville has Turnover with Nothing and Trauma Ray on Tuesday, Dec. 1, and Thursday, with Titus Andronicus, on Saturday, Jan. 23. Up the road in Lincoln, Bally's Event Center has a winter and spring of classic and hard rock booked, from Grand Funk Railroad on Jan. 30 to Gene Simmons on March 13, according to Songkick's venue listings.

The near one: Turnover at Fete, Dec. 1

The first big date comes in nine days. Turnover headlines Fete Music Hall on Tuesday, Dec. 1, at 7:30 p.m., with Nothing and Trauma Ray in support, according to the Concerts50 event listing. That listing places Turnover in Virginia Beach and Trauma Ray in Fort Worth, Texas, and tags the bill across indie, emo and shoegaze.

Fete sits at 103 Dike St. in Providence's Olneyville neighborhood. Songkick lists the room's capacity at 930 and describes it as a boutique live music venue. Ticket prices were not shown on the listings reviewed for this story, so check fetemusic.com before you go.

Fete's winter: Winter Solstice, then Thursday

Fete's December doesn't stop with Turnover. Songkick lists a Winter Solstice show at the hall on Saturday, Dec. 19, with no supporting acts named yet.

Then the calendar jumps into the new year. Thursday plays Fete on Saturday, Jan. 23, 2027, with Titus Andronicus on the bill, per Songkick. Songkick also lists Big Shrimp on Saturday, Feb. 6, and Babyfxce E on Sunday, Feb. 21.

Lincoln goes classic and hard rock

Bally's Event Center, at 100 Twin River Road in Lincoln, has put together a run of rock shows for the first months of 2027. Songkick's venue page lists these dates:

That is '70s arena rock with Grand Funk Railroad, an '80s metal voice with Sebastian Bach, the Kiss co-founder Gene Simmons out on his own, and '90s radio rock with Collective Soul. Wilson Phillips closes the list on April 17 with something softer. Songkick's page says seven concerts are on the venue's calendar, but only these five appeared in the listing reviewed for this story, so a couple more may already be booked. No opening acts or prices were shown.

Why it matters

Most of Rhode Island's rock news this time of year is about December's holiday shows. These listings look further out, and both rooms are leaning into guitars. For club fans, Fete's pairing of Turnover in December and Thursday in January makes for a strong winter. For anyone who grew up on classic rock radio, Lincoln's lineup is a reason to mark the calendar now.

How to go

Start with Turnover on Dec. 1, the only one of these shows inside the next few weeks. For tickets and start times, go to fetemusic.com for the Fete shows and to the Bally's Twin River Lincoln site or Ticketmaster for the Event Center. Dates can change, so check with the venue before you buy, and watch both calendars: more 2027 shows are likely to be added.
