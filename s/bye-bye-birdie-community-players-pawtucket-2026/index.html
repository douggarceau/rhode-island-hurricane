The Community Players Open Their Season With Bye Bye Birdie in Pawtucket

The Community Players open their season with Bye Bye Birdie at Jenks Auditorium in Pawtucket, with performances Nov. 13 to 22; dates vary, so check showtimes.

The Community Players, a Pawtucket theatre group now in its 104th season, opens its fall run with Bye Bye Birdie. The listing shows performances from Nov. 13 through Nov. 22 at Jenks Auditorium on Division Street, with the dates varying within that stretch.

About the Show

Bye Bye Birdie is a musical with a book by Michael Stewart, music by Charles Strouse and lyrics by Lee Adams. It opened on Broadway in 1960 and follows a rock star who is drafted into the Army. Before he leaves, his manager plans a farewell broadcast that includes a kiss from a small-town fan chosen through a contest. The show is known for its bright songs and its playful look at teenage life in the late 1950s.

Tickets and Times

Showtimes vary across the run, so plan ahead. Tickets are sold through the group's own ticketing page. For showtimes and tickets, call the group at (401) 726-6860 or write to info@thecommunityplayers.org.

About the Group

The Community Players stage four shows a year, two musicals and two non-musicals. The group describes itself as the longest-running community theatre in Rhode Island.

Plan Your Visit

Jenks Auditorium is at 350 Division Street in Pawtucket. The run spans two weekends, with the listed dates falling between Friday, Nov. 13 and Sunday, Nov. 22.
