Rhode Island Government, Mapped: Every Office on One Page

A new Rhode Island Hurricane guide lays out the whole system, from the governor's office to the last town hall.

Rhode Island's government is spread across dozens of websites that are hard to search and often out of date. Rhode Island Hurricane has put the whole system in one place: RI Government, Mapped.

What's in it

The guide covers 54 state agencies and offices with 171 divisions, the five statewide elected officers, 112 state legislators, six courts and 15 courthouses, 13 state authorities such as RIPTA and RIHousing, Rhode Island's four members of Congress, all 39 cities and towns, and 505 state boards and commissions.

Each entry shows who runs it, what it does, its main phone line, address and website, and its budget and headcount where the state publishes them.

How it's organized

Everything opens as drop-downs: branch, then group, then agency, then divisions, staff and workforce. A search box finds any office, town or name.

The guide is independent and privately run. It is not a State of Rhode Island website, and every fact links to the official source it came from.
