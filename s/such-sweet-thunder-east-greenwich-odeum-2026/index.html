East Greenwich jazz students play Ellington at the Greenwich Odeum

Wednesday, Nov. 18 at 7:30 p.m. at the Greenwich Odeum, 59 Main Street, East Greenwich; doors at 6:30 p.m., no charge.

On Wednesday, Nov. 18, the East Greenwich High School Jazz Ensemble takes the stage at the Greenwich Odeum for an evening of Duke Ellington and Billy Strayhorn. The concert, called Such Sweet Thunder, A Night of Ellington, starts at 7:30 p.m. Doors open at 6:30 p.m., and admission is no charge.

The Odeum at 59 Main Street in East Greenwich is a fitting room for a big-band program. The student players bring a set that many listeners know by heart, and the evening gives the ensemble a public showcase for the work it has been rehearsing this fall.

The Program

The set draws on the music Ellington and Strayhorn wrote together. Listeners can expect the bright swing of Take the A Train, the warm pulse of Caravan and the rich harmony of Isfahan. The evening also includes Black and Tan Fantasy and more pieces from the Essentially Ellington repertoire.

The title piece, Such Sweet Thunder, gives the concert its name.

Plan Your Evening

The event page does not list parking or seating details, so arriving a little early is a sensible plan. For questions, the Odeum box office can be reached at 401-885-4000. For a mid-November Wednesday night, it is a chance to hear a student jazz band tackle music by two of the genre's most celebrated writers.
