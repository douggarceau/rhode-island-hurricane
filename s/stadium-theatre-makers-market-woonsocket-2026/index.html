Local Makers Fill the Stadium Theatre for a Woonsocket Holiday Market

Saturday, Nov. 14, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., at the Stadium Theatre, 28 Monument Square, Woonsocket.

Local artists and creators gather at the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket on Saturday, Nov. 14, for a makers market running from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. Early entry at 11 a.m. costs $12 for VIP ticket holders, and general admission at 12 or 2 p.m. costs $8.

What's inside

The market is presented as a fundraiser for the arts programs of the Stadium Theatre Performing Arts Centre and its conservatory. The day includes raffles, prize games, a community book swap and a take-a-book, leave-a-book table, alongside the local makers' tables.

Why go

It is a chance to shop local work in one of Woonsocket's landmark venues, and to see inside a building that hosts concerts, stage shows and classes through the year. Check the venue's listing for any schedule changes before you go.
