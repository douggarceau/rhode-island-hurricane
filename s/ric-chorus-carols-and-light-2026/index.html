RIC Chorus Sings Carols and Light in Sapinsley Hall on Nov. 13

Friday, Nov. 13 at 7:30 p.m. in Sapinsley Hall at the Nazarian Center, 115 First Avenue, Providence, with paid admission.

The Rhode Island College Chorus opens the holiday season with Carols and Light, a concert in Sapinsley Hall at the Nazarian Center for the Performing Arts on Friday, Nov. 13, at 7:30 p.m. The hall is on the college campus at 115 First Avenue in Providence, and admission is paid.

The chorus builds its fall programs around choral music of several kinds. At the November concert last year, the program included Imant Raminsh's Magnificat, the traditional gospel-blues piece John the Revelator, Morten Lauridsen's O Nata Lux and a group of carols. Dr. Donald St. Jean directed, and Philip Martorella was the pianist.

Lauridsen's O Nata Lux sets a Latin text that fits the season, and carols sung by a college choir in a concert hall make a distinctive listening experience. It is a chance to hear a student ensemble at close range in a room built for the sound of voices.

Tickets and questions

Tickets for the concert are sold through the college's ticketing page. The Sapinsley Hall box office handles tickets for Sapinsley Hall, Roberts Auditorium and The Forman Theatre, and it can be reached at boxoffice@ric.edu or 401-456-8144, which makes it a good first call for seating questions.

The college's events page lists the program and ticket link for each concert, so check it for any changes before you go.
