Blithewold's Expressions of Nature Reception and Fall Family Days

Family programs on Saturday, Nov. 7 and an Expressions of Nature reception on Sunday, Nov. 15, 1 to 3 p.m., at Blithewold in Bristol.

Blithewold, the Bristol estate and arboretum, has a busy first half of November with family-friendly programs and a reception for its Expressions of Nature exhibit. The listing shows the exhibit running from Oct. 31 through Jan. 1. This year marks 100 years since Blithewold was recognized as an arboretum by scientists from the Arnold Arboretum, according to the Bristol Art Museum's spring show notes on the estate's site.

Saturday, Nov. 7: outdoors and mindful

Discover Blithewold Differently: A Mindful Outdoor Experience runs from 9 to 11 a.m. Marvelous Maples, presented by Joe Verstandig, takes place from 11 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. The listing gives times for both but does not describe the activities, so check with Blithewold for details before you go.

Sunday, Nov. 15: the Expressions of Nature reception

The exhibit's opening reception is listed from 1 to 3 p.m. on Nov. 15, a chance to see the work in person on a Sunday afternoon.

More listings through November

Nature Discovery Backpacks run through Nov. 22 from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. A Woodland Decoration Workshop with Terry Converse meets on Nov. 21 from 10 a.m. to noon. The programs page also lists a Holiday Jazz Tea on Nov. 28 from 11 a.m. to 1 p.m., and Sing-Along With Santa runs from Nov. 30 to Dec. 14. Prices are not listed for these programs, so check the Blithewold site before you plan a visit.
