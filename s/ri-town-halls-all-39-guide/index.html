Who Runs Your Town Hall: All 39 Rhode Island Cities and Towns

Town hall addresses, main lines and leaders for every community, with clerk, tax and police lines a free account away.

Every Rhode Island city and town runs its own website, and finding the right number can take longer than the call. The new Rhode Island Hurricane government guide lists all 39 in one place.

Free for everyone

Each town's entry shows its town or city hall, the address, the main phone line, the mayor or town manager, and the official website.

Free with a reader account

Create a free account and each town also shows its clerk's office line, the tax assessor and tax collector, the police non-emergency number, office hours, property tax rates and the adopted town budget where the town publishes it.

Search tip

Type a town name in the guide's search box. "Block Island" brings up New Shoreham.
