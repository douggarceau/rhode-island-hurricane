What 16 Years of State Payroll Reveal

Following 50,000 people through the state payroll shows patterns no agency publishes.

The state publishes its payroll one year at a time. Rhode Island Hurricane followed 50,010 people through 16 years of those files, from 2011 to now, and matched them against the budget.

What the findings cover

How fast management titles grew compared with the workforce since 2011. What share of all overtime goes to the top 10% of overtime earners. Whether hiring would cost less than overtime at the agencies with the most of it. Which agencies are losing staff fastest. How much of each agency has been on the payroll since 2011 and could retire soon. And how the jobs the budget pays for compare with the people actually paid.
