Newport Classical's November Nights: a Piano Debut and Guitar

Fridays, Nov. 6 and Nov. 20, at 7:30 p.m. at Newport Classical Recital Hall, 42 Dearborn Street, in Newport.

Newport Classical's chamber series keeps a steady Friday rhythm this fall, and two November evenings show how varied the programs can be. Both concerts start at 7:30 p.m. in the Recital Hall at 42 Dearborn Street in Newport, in the downtown area.

Nov. 6: a Newport debut at the piano

Pianist Michael Leonardo Davidman makes his Newport debut on Friday, Nov. 6. His program draws on J.S. Bach, Franz Schubert, César Franck and Franz Liszt, moving from the Baroque era into the Romantic. It is a good way to hear how one instrument can speak in very different voices. A debut also gives local listeners a chance to hear a new voice in the hall.

Nov. 20: guitar from Spain and Latin America

Guitarist Raphaël Feuillâtre brings a program called Latin Passions on Friday, Nov. 20. The concert surveys Spanish and Latin American repertoire by composers including Francisco Tárrega, Isaac Albéniz, Astor Piazzolla and Heitor Villa-Lobos. Those names suggest a varied evening, from the Spanish guitar tradition to the tango writing of Piazzolla.

The season behind the concerts

The 2026–27 chamber series includes twelve concerts from September 2026 through June 2027, according to the season announcement. Earlier in the season, the series presented a piano trio and a string quartet making its Newport Classical debut. Ticket prices and seating details are not on that announcement, so check the Newport Classical listing before you go.
