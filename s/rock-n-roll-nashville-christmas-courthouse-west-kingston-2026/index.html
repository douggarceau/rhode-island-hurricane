Rockabilly Under the Tree: Nashville Christmas Rolls Into West Kingston Dec. 11

The Courthouse Center for the Arts brings back its country-meets-rock 'n' roll holiday show on Friday, Dec. 11, with tickets at $25.

Rhode Island's holiday season gets a twang and a backbeat in South County next month. Rock N Roll Nashville Christmas returns to the Courthouse Center for the Arts in West Kingston on Friday, Dec. 11, according to the event's Visit Rhode Island listing, which bills it with the line: "The Courthouse Welcomes Back this Great Christmas Special!"

What it is

The show takes familiar Christmas songs and runs them through a country filter with rock 'n' roll energy. The listing promises "some of your favorite Christmas songs country style" and teases a few special guest appearances, jokingly name-checking Elvis, Roy O. and S. Claus. It calls the night a "Great Holiday Show for all."

The details

According to Visit Rhode Island, the show runs from 7 to 9:30 p.m. on Dec. 11 at the Courthouse Center for the Arts, 3481 Kingstown Road in West Kingston. Admission is listed at $25. The listing says the show is all ages, with no outside food or drink. The box office phone number on the listing is (401) 782-1018.

Who is behind it

This is a return engagement: the venue's own wording frames the show as a holiday special it is welcoming back. The listing does not name the individual musicians.

Why it matters

December in Rhode Island is crowded with Nutcrackers, Messiahs and orchestra pops nights. This one is for the crowd that would rather hear sleigh bells over a slapback guitar. Think of it as the holiday mixtape your favorite uncle would make if he owned a Telecaster and a pair of blue suede shoes. It is also a South County night out that does not require a drive to Providence or a big-room ticket price, and the all-ages policy makes it an easy family pick.

More December rock beyond Providence

Songkick's Providence-area rock calendar lists a few more December nights outside the capital. Pure Prairie League plays the Greenwich Odeum in East Greenwich the same night, Dec. 11, and Will Dailey is listed at Myrtle in East Providence on Friday, Dec. 18.

Road trip watch

Looking further out, Grace Potter and The Trespassers announced a 48-date 2027 U.S. tour in support of Trespasser, TicketNews reported Nov. 11. The run does not include a Rhode Island date. The closest stops are Boston's House of Blues on Saturday, Jan. 16, and College Street Music Hall in New Haven on Sunday, Jan. 17, both with Pokey LaFarge, according to the tour list. TicketNews reported that general on-sale began Nov. 13 through Ticketmaster and Potter's website.

How to go

Tickets for Rock N Roll Nashville Christmas are available through the Courthouse Center for the Arts; the Visit Rhode Island listing links to the box office. Because it is an all-ages Friday show at a smaller venue, buying ahead is the safe move.
