LeAnn Rimes Brings an Acoustic Night to Woonsocket's Stadium Theatre

Saturday, Nov. 14 at 8 p.m. at the Stadium Theatre, 28 Monument Square, Woonsocket, with tickets from $69 to $99.

Among the larger concerts on this weekend's calendar, LeAnn Rimes brings her acoustic show to the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket tonight at 8 p.m. The theatre describes her as an internationally acclaimed, multi-platinum-selling artist whose career spans nearly three decades.

The theatre's listing cites 21 albums, 71 radio singles and more than 48 million records sold, along with two Grammy Awards from eight nominations. It also points to her holiday single, "All I Want for Christmas Is You," and to her role on ABC's 9-1-1: Nashville, as well as her work as a Mega Mentor on The Voice.

Tickets and doors

Tickets are $69, $79, $89 and $99. The listing does not state a doors time, but the box office opens an hour and a half before the show starts. Balcony tickets are sold at the door when available.

The Stadium Theatre is at 28 Monument Square in Woonsocket. The theatre notes that it is not responsible for fulfillment or refunds on purchases made through third-party websites, so buying from the theatre's own box office is the safest route.

The listing does not describe the format of the acoustic performance beyond its title, so the best way to learn what the evening holds is to check the theatre's event page before you head out.
