Route 10 Traffic Shifts Begin in Providence and Cranston as Interchange Rebuild Moves On

RIDOT is moving Route 10 North onto a new bridge over Elmwood Avenue, with more lane changes and a weekend I-95 closure planned before the end of the year.

Drivers heading north on Route 10 between Cranston and Providence are being moved onto a newly built bridge over Elmwood Avenue starting Wednesday, Nov. 18, the first in a series of traffic changes the Rhode Island Department of Transportation has scheduled at the busy I-95 and Route 10 interchange over the next month. The department announced the changes in a travel advisory on Nov. 17 and said the new pattern on Route 10 North will stay in place until spring 2027.

What is changing now

According to RIDOT, Route 10 North traffic coming from Park Avenue will shift to the left just before Elmwood Avenue and cross the new bridge. Drivers who want I-95 North at Exit 1B should use the right lane. Drivers headed to I-95 South at Exit 1C, or continuing north on Route 10 toward Providence, should use the left lane. Traffic entering Route 10 from Elmwood Avenue will need to choose the same lanes. The department said it will put up signs and paint new pavement markings to guide drivers.

More changes on the calendar

The Elmwood Avenue shift is one of several steps RIDOT listed for the coming weeks.

For the mid-December weekend overnight closure of I-95, RIDOT advised drivers to plan to use alternate routes such as I-295 and Route 10. The department said it will release details once the schedule is confirmed.

Why the work is happening

The changes are part of RIDOT's I-95 15 Bridges project, which replaces or repairs 15 bridges along I-95 and Route 10 between Providence and Warwick. According to the department, nine of those bridges are rated structurally deficient, and three are among the five most heavily traveled structurally deficient bridges in Rhode Island. At the Huntington Viaduct, RIDOT is redesigning the entire Route 10 and I-95 interchange.

Part of Route 10 South between Elmwood Avenue and Park Avenue is being rebuilt as a boulevard-style street rather than a highway, the department said. The Dec. 6 shift will make room for a new median barrier along that stretch.

What it means for drivers

RIDOT said the changes are temporary but may add travel time. The new lane rules near Elmwood Avenue matter most for commuters who use Route 10 to reach I-95, since drivers will need to be in the correct lane earlier than before. Drivers who regularly travel I-95 South through Cranston and Warwick will also see the separate lane split starting Friday night, Nov. 20, near the Jefferson Boulevard exit, tied to the replacement of the bridges carrying I-95 over the Pawtuxet River and Jefferson Boulevard.

What comes next

The department said construction is subject to changes in schedule and scope. Project updates are posted on RIDOT's website at dot.ri.gov and on the I-95 15 Bridges project page.
