Almost, Maine Brings Northern Lights Romance to West Warwick

Evening shows at 7:30 p.m. and Sunday matinees at 2 p.m. through Nov. 22 at The Arctic Playhouse in West Warwick.

The Arctic Playhouse in West Warwick is staging John Cariani's Almost, Maine this November, a gentle play about love arriving in unexpected places.

A town that may not be on the map

According to the production listing, the play takes place in a remote northern town that is nearly in Canada and may not formally exist. Its residents are caught up in romance and heartbreak as the northern lights shine overhead. Because the story is told through separate scenes, each pair of characters gets a moment of its own, and the evening moves from tender to surprising to wistful.

Dates and tickets

Evening performances begin at 7:30 p.m. on Thursday, Nov. 5; Friday, Nov. 6; Saturday, Nov. 7; Friday and Saturday, Nov. 13 and 14; and Thursday through Saturday, Nov. 19 to 21. Sunday matinees start at 2 p.m. on Nov. 8, 15 and 22.

Tickets are listed at $22. The theatre is at 1249 Main Street in West Warwick, and the listing gives the production's ticket price for every date in the run.

Who is behind it

The production is directed by W. Richard Johnson. The listing does not name the cast, so anyone curious about the actors should check with the theatre before a visit. For a November evening with a little winter magic, this is a good pick for a night out with friends or a date night.
