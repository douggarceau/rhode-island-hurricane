Christmas at Blithewold Brings Nature Displays and Sparkle Lights

Tuesdays through Sundays, 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. through Jan. 3, with evening Sparkle lights from 4 p.m., at Blithewold in Bristol.

Blithewold, the historic mansion, gardens and arboretum on Ferry Road in Bristol, has opened its holiday season, Christmas at Blithewold. The celebration runs from Nov. 24 through Jan. 3. The property is open Tuesday through Sunday and closed on Mondays, and it also closes for Thanksgiving Day, Christmas Eve and Christmas Day.

Gifts of Nature

This year's Manor theme is Gifts of Nature. Displays across the house show birds, butterflies, woodland creatures, mushrooms, flowers, stars and storybook scenes, and elaborate displays appear in every corner of the house.

Sparkle After Dark

From 4 p.m., the Manor is dimmed and lit with thousands of ornaments, while the gardens glow with handmade bamboo ornaments. On Friday and Saturday nights the grounds add fire pits, hot cocoa and s'mores, and Santa may visit. S'mores kits are sold separately. Guests who arrive in daylight can stay for the lights by paying the difference at the door.

Tickets and Tea

Daytime admission for adults is $22, with discounts by age or affiliation, and tickets are bought on site. Sparkle evening tickets include the Manor and Gardens: nonmembers pay $27 and members pay $15, while children ages 6 to 17 pay $8 as nonmembers or $5 as members. Children 5 and under enter at no charge. Last entry is at 7 p.m., and the property closes at 8 p.m. Afternoon tea is served in the Dining Room, with reservations made online.
