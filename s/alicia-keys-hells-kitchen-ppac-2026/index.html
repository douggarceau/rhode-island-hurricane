Alicia Keys' Hell's Kitchen Runs at PPAC Dec. 15 Through 20

Tuesday, Dec. 15 through Sunday, Dec. 20 at the Providence Performing Arts Center, 220 Weybosset Street, Providence, with evening and weekend matinee performances.

The Broadway musical Alicia Keys' Hell's Kitchen comes to the Providence Performing Arts Center for six days in December as part of the 2026-27 Broadway Series. Inspired by the music and life of singer and songwriter Alicia Keys, the show follows a seventeen-year-old in the New York neighborhood of its title and blends Keys' songs into a new story.

Performances run Tuesday, Dec. 15 and Wednesday, Dec. 16 at 7 p.m.; Thursday, Dec. 17 and Friday, Dec. 18 at 7:30 p.m.; Saturday, Dec. 19 at 1 p.m. and 7:30 p.m.; and Sunday, Dec. 20 at 1 p.m. and 6:30 p.m. A BankNewport Family Night takes place Wednesday, Dec. 16 at 7 p.m. with specially priced tickets.

The show is one of six productions in PPAC's 2026-27 Broadway Series, which also includes Death Becomes Her, The Notebook and Maybe Happy Ending later in the season. Subscription packages are on sale now, and the venue says individual on-sale dates have not yet been announced, so check ppacri.org or the box office for the latest information.

Getting there

The Providence Performing Arts Center is at 220 Weybosset Street, Providence, RI 02903. The box office is open Monday through Friday from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. and Saturday from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m., and it also opens two hours before curtain on performance days. Groups of ten or more can call 401.421.ARTS (2787) or email groups@ppacri.org.
