Handel's Messiah with the RI Philharmonic and Providence Singers

A family-friendly Sunday matinee on Dec. 13, 3 p.m., at The VETS in Providence, with tickets from $25.

Handel's Messiah, the faith-themed oratorio that has become a December tradition across the English-speaking world, comes to Providence on Sunday, December 13, 2026, when the Rhode Island Philharmonic Orchestra joins the Providence Singers at the Veterans Memorial Auditorium. Doors open at 2:00 p.m. and the performance begins at 3:00 p.m.

The artists

Christine Noel conducts. She is also artistic director of the Providence Singers, the chorus joining the orchestra for this performance. The solo quartet named by the Philharmonic is soprano Maya Kherani, mezzo-soprano Emily Marvosh, tenor Jonas Budris and baritone Andrew Garland. Season materials issued earlier listed a slightly different vocal line-up, so it is worth confirming the final roster with the box office.

A matinee for families

The Philharmonic bills the afternoon as a family-friendly Sunday matinee. Messiah is long and rich, but its great choruses and arias reward listeners of any age, and the afternoon start time is kinder to young audiences than an evening concert.

The program is a clean fit for the season: a work built from scripture, scored for orchestra, chorus and four soloists, and sung in a hall that can hold the full sound of both.

Venue and tickets

The VETS is at 1 Avenue of the Arts, Providence, RI 02903. Tickets start at $25 and are on sale now through tickets.riphil.org or by phone at the RIPO Box Office, 401-248-7000. The orchestra notes that the VETS box office cannot sell Philharmonic tickets or change existing orders.

It is a classic way to mark the start of the holiday season, and just days before The Nutcracker arrives in the same hall.
