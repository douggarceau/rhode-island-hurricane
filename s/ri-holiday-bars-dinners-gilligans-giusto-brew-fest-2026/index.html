Sleigh-Saurus on Chalkstone: Rhode Island's Holiday Bars and Big Dinners

A pop-up holiday bar in Providence, a Newport lasagna that feeds two families, Champagne and whisky at The Vanderbilt and a Brew Fest on deck for January.

The holiday bar is back, and Rhode Island's restaurants are going all in. Rhode Island Monthly's latest Diner's Update, published Nov. 25 by Jamie Coelho, rounds up the season's themed cocktails, chef takeovers and wine dinners from Providence to Newport to Narragansett. Here is where to drink and eat between now and New Year's.

The headliner: Miracle on Chalkstone

Gilligan's Pub in Providence has turned itself into the "Miracle on Chalkstone" holiday bar, running Nov. 20 through Dec. 31, according to the magazine. The themed cocktail list carries names like Sugarplum Stampede, Sleigh-Saurus and Merry Mouse-Tail.

Downtown, Esther's High Dive has decked itself out for the season, as has Stingy Alley in Warren, per the update. And both Providence locations of Harry's Bar & Burger are running holiday decor and a Golden Ticket giveaway, with "12 Days of Christmas" specials Dec. 7 through 18 that include fifty-cent wings and half-price milkshakes.

Newport: lasagna for a cause and a chef takeover

Giusto in Newport is back with its Buy One, Give One Lasagna for a sixth year, Rhode Island Monthly reports. Each lasagna feeds six to eight people and costs $75 plus tax, and the program's donations go to the MLK Center in Newport. Orders are taken by phone at 401-324-7400 from 1 to 6 p.m., with pickups running through Dec. 23.

Giusto's kitchen also takes over Mother Pizzeria in Newport on Wednesday, Dec. 2, from 5 to 9 p.m., per the update.

The Vanderbilt in Newport has a Champagne dinner with Billecart-Salmon on Dec. 2, then runs "12 Days of The Macallan" Thursday through Sunday from Dec. 3 to Dec. 26, the magazine says. It is also planning a black-tie New Year's Eve event with an open bar.

Wine nights and a lesson in a glass

Red Stripe is hosting a four-course Gooseneck Vineyards wine dinner on Thursday, Dec. 3, at 6 p.m. for $89 per person, according to Rhode Island Monthly; reservations are at redstriperestaurants.com or 401-437-6950. The same night, Track 15, the downtown Providence food hall, holds another session of its Sip & Learn series at 6 p.m. for $50 per person, with one more on Dec. 16.

Sakonnet Vineyard hosts Shop, Sip and Visit on Saturday, Dec. 5, from noon to 4 p.m., with Santa on hand from 2 to 3 p.m. and a toy drive for the Tiverton Police Department, per the update.

South County brunches

In Narragansett, Celeste is serving Breakfast with Santa on Sunday, Dec. 6, from 8 to 10:30 a.m., at $5 for children and $7 for adults, benefiting the Narragansett Lions Club, according to the magazine. In Richmond, The Preserve has holiday brunches on Sundays, Dec. 6, 13 and 20, with wagon rides, weather permitting. The Dec. 6 and 20 brunches seat every half hour from 8 to 11:30 a.m. at $30 per person; Dec. 13 is a Santa brunch from 10 a.m. to 2 p.m. at $79 per adult and $30 per child. Reservations are at 401-539-0300.

What comes next

Beer people can look past the holidays. The Rhode Island Brew Fest returns Saturday, Jan. 30, 2027, at WaterFire Providence's building in Providence, with two sessions, the magazine reports. Tickets are $60 in advance and $70 at the door, with designated-driver tickets at $20 in advance and $25 at the door.

And if you have a favorite spot, there is still time to say so: public voting for the Rhode Island Hospitality Association's ANCHORS Awards runs through Nov. 30, per Rhode Island Monthly, ahead of the awards night on Jan. 12, 2027, at Bally's Twin River in Lincoln.
