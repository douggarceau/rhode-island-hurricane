Jamestown Arts Center Opens Its 2026 Members' Show

Friday, Nov. 20, 5:30 to 7:30 p.m., at 18 Valley Street in Jamestown; on view through Jan. 9, 2027.

The Jamestown Arts Center opens its Members' Show on Friday, Nov. 20, with a reception from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. at the gallery at 18 Valley Street. The exhibition runs through Jan. 9, 2027, which gives visitors most of the winter to see it.

Who is showing

The show brings together work from the Arts Center's community and beyond, according to the center's page. It includes first-time exhibitors, emerging artists and established names, so a single visit can show how a local arts community looks from many directions at once.

The judge

The guest judge is Maria Fernanda Mancera, assistant curator of Indigenous art at the Rhode Island School of Design. The Arts Center's page also recalls that last year's Best-in-Show winner was Molly K. Dickinson. Her current show, Of Longing and Loss, is on view at the center this season as well.

Visiting

Gallery hours are Wednesday through Saturday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., and by appointment. The page does not list an admission charge. The opening reception is a good way to start the season's show, and the gallery's hours make a weekday or Saturday visit easy to plan.
