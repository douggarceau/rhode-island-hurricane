Warren's Holiday Festival Brings a Full Friday of Family Fun, Nov. 27

Friday, Nov. 27, from 2:30 to 9 p.m. in downtown Warren, with the Main Street lighting at 7:15 p.m. at Town Hall.

Warren's holiday season opens on Friday, Nov. 27, with the Warren Holiday Festival, a town-wide evening in the historic downtown where nearly every event is open to the public at no charge. The schedule lets visitors move from the waterfront to Main Street over one afternoon and evening.

Start the afternoon with the children

Events begin at 2:30 p.m. at FairyTea on Market Street, where Santa reads 'Twas the Night Before Christmas and families join a holiday sing-along. At about 5 p.m. Santa arrives at the foot of Baker Street on a decorated firetruck and leads children to the Firemen's Museum at 38 Baker St. to hear their wishes.

Shows, music and carolers

Marvelous Marvin Magic performs two all-ages shows at 5:15 and 6 p.m. Circus Dynamics brings fire spinners and stunts behind Washington Lodge No. 3 at 39 Baker St. at 5:15 and 6:15 p.m. At 5:30 p.m. the historian and musician Richard Donohue presents Christmas songs spanning several centuries at Maxwell House on Church Street. The Mt. Hope High School Vocal Ensemble sings at 6 p.m. at First Methodist Church, 25 Church St., and the Very Merry Dickens Carolers perform at Town Hall at 6 p.m. before strolling Main Street.

The lighting ceremony

The evening's centerpiece is the Main Street Light Ceremony at 7:15 p.m. at Town Hall, 514 Main St. Guests join a sing-along, hear brief remarks from the festival committee, and count down to the tree lighting. Strolling performers work Water and Baker streets through the evening.

Around town and before you go

First Methodist Church serves hot chocolate, cider and cookies, with ornament crafts for children downstairs. The Federal Blues R.I.M. carriage house at 42 Baker St. hosts an open house with militia members in colonial dress. Bring a new, unwrapped toy for the festival's Fill-a-Trunk drive for children in need, and check the festival website for the full schedule and a trolley stops map before you go.
