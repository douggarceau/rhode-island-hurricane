Newport Art Museum Reunites Paintings by Howard Gardiner Cushing

Open Wednesday through Monday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., in the Cushing Gallery at the Newport Art Museum in Newport, through Dec. 31, 2027.

The Newport Art Museum has opened Howard Gardiner Cushing: Rediscoveries, a show that brings together paintings by an artist whose work the museum holds in the largest public collection. Many of the pictures come from private and institutional lenders, and some have rarely or never been shown to the public.

A Painter Trained in Paris

Cushing lived from 1869 to 1916 and studied at the Académie Julian in Paris, where he built a foundation in academic technique. Influenced by James McNeill Whistler and the Aesthetic Movement, he cared more about beauty, design and atmosphere than about delivering a message. His output included portraits, atmospheric genre scenes, decorative compositions, mural projects and imaginative tableaux.

Following a Strong Season

The new gallery show follows the museum's 2025 exhibition, Howard Gardiner Cushing: A Harmony of Line and Color, and presents paintings that have been reunited for this display. The earlier show focused on the artist's line and color, and this one adds paintings that have not been seen together before.

Visiting the Gallery

The Cushing Gallery is open 11 a.m. to 4 p.m. Wednesday through Monday and closed on Tuesdays. Museum members, visitors 17 and under, and active military personnel with a spouse enter at no charge. Adult admission is $20, seniors 65 and older pay $15, and college students pay $10. The museum sits at 76 Bellevue Avenue in Newport, and the show stays up through Dec. 31, 2027, so there is time to plan a visit for the holidays or the new year.
