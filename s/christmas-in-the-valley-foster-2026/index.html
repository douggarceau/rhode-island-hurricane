Christmas in the Valley Brings Fine Art and Craft to Foster

Friday, Nov. 27 through Sunday, Nov. 29 at the Foster Country Club, 67 Johnson Road, Foster, with fine art and fine craft exhibitors.

Christmas in the Valley is a holiday show that brings fine art and fine craft exhibitors together at the Foster Country Club, 67 Johnson Road in Foster. The show runs Friday, Nov. 27 through Sunday, Nov. 29, the weekend after Thanksgiving, when many families are looking for an outing close to home.

The emphasis is on handmade work rather than food. The event is listed with no food booths, which makes it a good stop for shoppers who want to browse at a relaxed pace and take their time at each exhibitor's table. Anyone looking for a gift can see a mix of fine art and fine craft in one place.

A raffle of items donated by exhibitors is part of the event, adding a little fun to the day. Those who want to take part can look for the raffle while they visit.

Foster is a small town in western Rhode Island, and an afternoon at the Foster Country Club makes an easy seasonal outing for neighbors and visitors alike. The event page does not list admission or hours, so check the show's official website before heading out.

Whether you are planning holiday gifts, looking for art for your home or simply enjoying a crisp November weekend in the country, Christmas in the Valley offers a warm, low-key way to begin the season. Bring a friend, make a day of it and plan to visit more than once.
