Bert Kreischer Brings Stand-Up to The VETS in Providence Tonight

Friday, Nov. 13 at 7 p.m. at Veterans Memorial Auditorium, 1 Avenue of the Arts, Providence; doors open at 6 p.m.

Tonight's largest non-sports show in the state brings one of the most successful stand-up comedians in the world to Providence. Bert Kreischer takes the stage at Veterans Memorial Auditorium, known as The VETS, for his current tour, with the curtain at 7 p.m. and doors opening at 6 p.m.

Kreischer began as a well-known personality with a loyal following and has since grown into one of the highest-grossing stand-up comics anywhere. He is also an entrepreneur, actor, producer and content creator, and critics have praised his candid, personal storytelling. Expect a show built on his own life and his way of telling a story from start to finish.

The VETS sits across from the State House in the heart of downtown Providence, at 1 Avenue of the Arts, Providence, RI 02903. Parking and public transit options are easiest to plan ahead, so allow extra time for a Friday evening arrival.

Tickets are sold online at thevets.evenue.net/events/BK26, by phone at (401) 421-2787, and in person at the PPAC Box Office at 220 Weybosset Street. The on-site VETS box office opens only on show days. Children under six are not admitted unless a show is designated a family show, so call the box office to confirm before bringing younger guests.
