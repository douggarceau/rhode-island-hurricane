Heritage Ballet Brings The Nutcracker to Woonsocket's Stadium Theatre

Heritage Ballet performs The Nutcracker at the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket on Sat., Nov. 28 at 2 and 7 p.m. and Sun., Nov. 29 at 2 p.m.

Heritage Ballet brings its full-length production of The Nutcracker to the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket for three performances at the end of November. The run gives families a chance to see the classic ballet close to home.

The Schedule

Performances are set for Saturday, Nov. 28 at 2 p.m. and 7 p.m., with a matinee on Sunday, Nov. 29 at 2 p.m. Tickets range from $20 to $45. Online orders carry a per-ticket service fee, and phone orders carry a smaller one, so check the checkout total before you buy.

The Story

The tale opens on Christmas, when Clara receives a toy Nutcracker from her godfather. That night the doll comes to life and battles the Mouse Queen. Clara then travels through a snowy forest with the Snow Queen and King and their Snowflakes, and arrives in the Land of Sweets, where she meets the Sugar Plum Fairy. The story closes by asking whether the whole journey was only a dream.

Where to Go

The Stadium Theatre sits at 28 Monument Square in Woonsocket. The Saturday and Sunday afternoon shows suit families planning a daytime outing, while the Saturday 7 p.m. performance offers a later start.

Before the Curtain

Heritage Ballet's staging follows the traditional sequence of the ballet, from the Christmas party and the battle scene through the snowy forest to the Land of Sweets.
