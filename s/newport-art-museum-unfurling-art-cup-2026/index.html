Unfurling: Newport Art Museum Previews Its 2027 Art Cup Sails

Through Dec. 31 at Griswold House, 76 Bellevue Ave., Newport, Monday, Wednesday through Saturday and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.

Newport Art Museum is showing a small but ambitious preview of its next big summer project. Unfurling: Small Work by the 2027 Art Cup Artists is on view at the museum's Griswold House, 76 Bellevue Avenue in Newport, through December 31, 2026.

The show previews the 2027 Art Cup, a one-off regatta off Fort Adams State Park in which contemporary artists' imagery will be printed on the sails of more than a dozen Shields sailboats. The museum organizes the project with Sail Newport and Shields Fleet 9, and each participating artist will create a new work for the sails.

Seven artists, small-scale works

This exhibition gathers small works by seven of the participating artists: Katherine Bradford, Bob Dilworth, Emil Lukas, Tomokazu Matsuyama, Odili Donald Odita, Kelsy Patnaude, and Coral Woodbury. Seeing studio-scale pieces by artists with very different approaches is a fitting way to think about the central question of the project, which is how an image changes when it is scaled up to fill a sail that will catch the wind on the water.

Visiting the museum

The museum is open Monday, Wednesday through Saturday, and Sunday from 11 a.m. to 4 p.m., and it is closed on Tuesdays. General admission is $20 for adults, $15 for seniors 65 and older, and $10 for college students. Members, children 17 and under, and active military members with their spouses are admitted at no charge.

Because the show stays up through the end of the year, it works well for a fall or winter visit. Visitors can call (401) 848-8200, email hello@newportartmuseum.org, or visit newportartmuseum.org for more information.
