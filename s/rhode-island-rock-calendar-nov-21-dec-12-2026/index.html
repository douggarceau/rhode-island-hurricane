Turn It Up: Three Weeks of Rock Shows Rolling Through Rhode Island

From a noise-rock blowout in Pawtucket to Less Than Jake at The Strand, the state's club calendar is loaded through Dec. 12.

The holidays may be coming, but Rhode Island's stages are not slowing down. Between tonight and Saturday, Dec. 12, rock and roots shows are booked from Pawtucket to South Kingstown to Cranston, according to Songkick's Providence-area rock listings, with ska-punk veterans Less Than Jake closing the stretch at The Strand in downtown Providence.

Tonight: Kafkaesque takes over The Met

The run starts Saturday, Nov. 21, with Kafkaesque Providence 2026 at The Met in Pawtucket. Songkick lists a bill built for adventurous ears: King Missile, Dropdead, Sunburned Hand of the Man, Weird Nightmare, PONS, Flesh Narc, Drunks With Guns and Baylies Band. The Met sits at 1005 Main St. in Pawtucket and holds about 500 people, according to its Songkick venue page. Start times and ticket prices were not shown on the listings reviewed for this story, so check themetri.com before heading out.

The big ones

For pure volume, two December dates stand out. On Tuesday, Dec. 1, Turnover plays Fete Music Hall with Nothing and Trauma Ray in support, per Songkick, a heavy three-band bill on a school night. Then on Saturday, Dec. 12, Less Than Jake brings what Visit Rhode Island's listing calls its "signature blend of ska punk and infectious melodies" to The Strand Ballroom and Theatre at 79 Washington St. The listing gives the show time as 7:30 p.m. and promises classic hits alongside newer songs. It does not name an opening act or list prices.

Down the road

Some of the best nights are outside the capital. Cranston's Historic Park Theatre has a three-show run in eight days: Chris Isaak on Dec. 3, The Wood Brothers with Tyler Ramsey on Dec. 4 and Cherry Poppin' Daddies on Dec. 11. In South County, I Am the Avalanche plays Ocean Mist in South Kingstown on Nov. 28, Thanksgiving weekend, with Classic Traffic opening. Pure Prairie League is at the Greenwich Odeum in East Greenwich on Dec. 11, and Rickie Lee Jones plays the Granite Theatre in Westerly on Dec. 12.

Back in Providence, Consider the Source plays The Parlour at 1119 N. Main St. on Friday, Dec. 11, at 9 p.m., according to JamBase, one stop on a short Northeast run that also hits New Hampshire, New York, New Jersey and Connecticut.

Why it matters

Even with the holidays closing in, national acts are still routing through mid-size Rhode Island venues, and the state's smaller clubs, from The Met to The Parlour, are filling the gaps with bills that would draw crowds in much bigger cities.

How to go

Songkick's listings are a snapshot and can change, so confirm dates, doors and prices with each venue before buying. The Met, Fete, The Strand, the Park Theatre, the Odeum, the Granite Theatre and The Parlour all sell tickets through their own websites.
