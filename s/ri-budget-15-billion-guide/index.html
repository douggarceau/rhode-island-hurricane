Where Rhode Island's $15.2 Billion Budget Goes

The FY2027 budget, agency by agency, in the new Rhode Island Hurricane government guide.

Rhode Island's budget for the year that began July 1 totals $15.24 billion in all funds, up from $14.34 billion the year before, according to the House Fiscal Advisory Staff's report on the budget as enacted.

About $6.08 billion of that is state general revenue, mostly taxes and fees. Another $5.73 billion comes from the federal government. The budget authorizes 15,997 full-time positions.

By area

Health and human services is the largest area at $6.90 billion, followed by education at $3.70 billion, general government at $2.40 billion, transportation at $1.15 billion, public safety at $907 million and natural resources at $187 million.

In the guide

Every agency card shows its total budget for free. A free reader account adds each agency's split between state taxes, federal money and other funds, its authorized staff, and the change from last year, plus a table of every agency from largest to smallest.
