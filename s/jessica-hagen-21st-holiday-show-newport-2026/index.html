Newport's Jessica Hagen Gallery Opens Its 21st Holiday Show

The annual holiday exhibition opens with a reception from 5 to 7 PM on Saturday, Nov. 21, at 9A Bridge Street in Newport.

Jessica Hagen Fine Art + Design on Bridge Street in Newport opens its 21st annual holiday show with a reception on Saturday, November 21, from 5 to 7 PM. The exhibition runs through December 31, 2026, and admission is at no charge.

What is on view

More than three dozen artists are represented in the show, with paintings, ceramics, jewelry and books. Because the exhibition brings many media together under one roof, it works equally well as a gallery visit and as a place to find original, handmade holiday gifts.

Visiting the gallery

The gallery is at 9A Bridge Street in Newport, RI 02840. Its listing gives regular hours as 11 AM to 5 PM, with different Sunday hours shown on the page, so it is worth checking before you go. Questions can go to the gallery by phone at (401) 835-7682, and the exhibition details are posted at jessicahagen.com/exhibitions.
