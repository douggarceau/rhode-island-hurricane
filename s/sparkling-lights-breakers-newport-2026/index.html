Sparkling Lights Opens at The Breakers in Newport on Nov. 21

Saturday, Nov. 21 opens the holiday evenings, with timed entries from 4 to 6 p.m. at The Breakers on Ochre Point Avenue in Newport.

Sparkling Lights at The Breakers, the holiday evening program at the Preservation Society of Newport County's Ochre Point Avenue mansion, opens on Saturday, Nov. 21. It continues on selected evenings through Dec. 27. The published calendar lists Nov. 21, 22, 27, 28 and 29, then Dec. 3 to 6, 10 to 13, 18, 20 to 23, and 26 and 27.

Timed entry

Every visitor needs a timed ticket for a set entry time. Entries run every half hour from 4:00 to 6:00 p.m., and the house and grounds close at 8:00 p.m. A timed ticket includes interior access to the mansion during the same visit. Regular Breakers tickets are not accepted after 3:00 p.m. on Sparkling Lights days.

Getting in

Guests enter through the main gates on Ochre Point Avenue at their scheduled time, or up to 30 minutes after it. Tickets are scanned on entry, and a ticket cannot be used to come back in later. Advance purchase is recommended. Tickets sold on the day are offered only if the event has not sold out.

Prices and member discounts

The event page does not list prices, so check the tickets page at newportmansions.org before you go. Standard members receive a discount on Sparkling Lights tickets, while NARM members do not. The Preservation Society lists 401-847-1000 for questions about tickets.

About the house

The Breakers was built as a summer home for the Vanderbilt family and is one of the Newport mansions the Preservation Society cares for. The Sparkling Lights evenings give visitors a chance to see its interior in the early evening of the holiday season.
