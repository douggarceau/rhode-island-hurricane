Vienna Boys Choir Brings Christmas in Vienna to Cranston

Saturday, Dec. 5 at 7:30 p.m. at the Historic Park Theatre, 848 Park Avenue in Cranston, with resale tickets listed from $114.

The Vienna Boys Choir, known in German as the Wiener Sängerknaben, brings its Christmas in Vienna program to Cranston on Saturday, December 5, at 7:30 p.m. The concert is at the Historic Park Theatre and Event Center, 848 Park Avenue.

The choir traces its origins to 1498, when Emperor Maximilian I established a boys' choir at the imperial chapel in Vienna. Centuries later the ensemble is one of the most recognized boys' choirs in the world, and its holiday concerts are built around the bright, precise sound of young voices.

The listing we checked describes the evening as a Christmas program without publishing the song list, so ask the box office for the repertoire if you want to know what will be sung. The page does make the basics clear: tickets were listed from $114 on a resale marketplace, with fees included and taxes extra, and resale prices can sit above face value.

For many listeners, a boys' choir at Christmas is a welcome change from the familiar holiday playlist. It suits families, music lovers and anyone who wants to start the season with a live performance that sounds a little different from the usual carols.

Visiting boys' choirs of this kind typically pair sacred and seasonal repertoire with solo features, and the discipline of the young singers is often the first thing audiences remark on. For families bringing children to the concert, it can be a memorable first step into choral music.
