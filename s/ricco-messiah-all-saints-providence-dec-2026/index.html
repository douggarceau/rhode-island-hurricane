RICCO Brings Handel's Messiah to All Saints in Providence

Saturday, Dec. 5, at 7 p.m. at All Saints Memorial Church in Providence, and Sunday, Dec. 6, at 3 p.m. at St. James Church in Manville.

The Rhode Island Civic Chorale and Orchestra, known as RICCO, will present Handel's Messiah twice this December. The first performance is Saturday, Dec. 5, at 7 p.m. at All Saints Memorial Church on Westminster Street in Providence. The second is Sunday, Dec. 6, at 3 p.m. at St. James Church in Manville. Artistic Director Joshua Rohde leads the RICCO chorale and an orchestra of professional musicians through the oratorio.

This year's concerts carry a first for the organization. The Brown University Chorus joins RICCO on stage, the first time the two groups have worked together. The soloists include winners of RICCO's Collegiate Vocal Competition, so the evening gives emerging young singers a place alongside the professional orchestra and the full chorale.

About the work

Handel composed Messiah in 1741 and it was first performed in Dublin in 1742. Its text is drawn from the King James Bible, and its best-known movement, the "Hallelujah" chorus, is one of the most recognized pieces in choral music. Every December, choirs across the region take it up as a seasonal tradition, and a performance in a church setting suits the work's sacred roots.

Where and when

The Providence concert takes place at All Saints Memorial Church, 674 Westminster St., Providence. The Manville concert takes place at St. James Church in Manville. The listing does not give a street address for the Manville church, so check the RICCO website before you go.

Tickets

For the Providence performance, tickets are $35 for adults and $15 for students. Those prices exclude fees. No pricing for the Manville concert was listed. Full details and tickets are available at ricco.org, the organization's website.
