All 113 seats were on the ballot. In the unofficial count, Democratic candidates lead in 36 of 38 state Senate districts and Republican candidates in 2. In the 75-seat House, Democratic candidates lead in 66 districts, Republican candidates in 8 and an independent candidate in 1. The state's results page marks two legislative races as close: Senate District 17 and House District 49.
