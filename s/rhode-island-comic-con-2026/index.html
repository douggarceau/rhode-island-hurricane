Rhode Island Comic Con Takes Over Downtown Providence, Nov. 6–8

John Cena, Christina Ricci and Mick Foley headline three days at the AMP, the Convention Center and the Omni. New this year: no smart glasses.

Rhode Island Comic Con returns Friday through Sunday, Nov. 6–8, spreading across the Amica Mutual Pavilion, the Rhode Island Convention Center and the Omni Providence Hotel.

Who's coming

John Cena appears Sunday, Nov. 8 only, and his photo and autograph slots are limited. Other featured guests include Christina Ricci, wrestling legend Mick Foley and four voice actors from the Sonic the Hedgehog games.

Some guests have canceled, including R.L. Stine, George Takei, John Ratzenberger, Ariel Winter and Dan Fogler. Organizers post changes at the bottom of the guests page at ricomiccon.com, so check before you buy a photo op.

What else is new

There's a 39-card trading card set sold only at the show, with some autographed cards mixed into packs. The Blockbuster Experience is back, a re-creation of an old video store with classic movies, for an added fee.

The rules

Smart glasses like Meta Ray-Bans and other wearable recording devices are banned this year. Anyone caught using them can be removed and lose their pass. The full code of conduct, including costume and prop rules, is at ricomiccon.com.

Tickets

Tickets are sold at the AMP box office, through Ticketmaster and at Toy Vault stores. The panel schedule hadn't been released as of this week; follow the show's Facebook page or the Altered Reality Entertainment app for updates.

Heads up: The Great Gatsby is playing PPAC the same weekend, so expect a busy downtown.
