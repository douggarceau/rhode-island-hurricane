Boston Pops Bring Holiday Favorites to PPAC in Providence on Dec. 13

Sunday, Dec. 13, at 7 p.m. at the Providence Performing Arts Center in downtown Providence.

The Boston Pops, the orchestra known for bright, crowd-pleasing programs, will bring a holiday concert to the Providence Performing Arts Center this December. Keith Lockhart leads the evening, which is built around seasonal favorites that suit families, longtime concertgoers and people who rarely attend an orchestra performance.

When and Where

The concert is set for Sunday, Dec. 13, at 7 p.m. The hall sits on Weybosset Street in downtown Providence, a short walk from the city's restaurants and shops, which makes it easy to pair the show with dinner before or drinks after.

Getting Tickets

Listings say tickets are sold through the PPAC box office and the ScoreBig ticketing service, and advance purchase is the surest way to get the seats you want. A ticketing news report from July said sales were already open and noted that the service adds no hidden fees to the ticket price.

Another Choice That Day

Dec. 13 also brings a second seasonal option to the capital. The Rhode Island Philharmonic lists its annual performance of Handel's Messiah at The VETS in Providence the same day. The Providence Singers join the orchestra for the choral work, with four vocal soloists, so music fans can compare a grand orchestral holiday program with a large-scale choral masterpiece in one weekend.
