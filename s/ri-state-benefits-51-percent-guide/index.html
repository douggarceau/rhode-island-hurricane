A State Job's Hidden Half: Benefits Add 51% to Salaries

What a state job comes with beyond the paycheck, from the budget office's own numbers.

The state budget for this year includes $1.63 billion in salaries for state employees and $838 million in benefits on top, according to the House Fiscal Advisory Staff. That's about 51% on top of salaries.

House Fiscal gives an example: a state employee earning $65,000 with family health coverage costs the state $121,077 a year in all.
