Museum Reads Turns to Paul Gauguin at the Newport Art Museum

Thursday, Nov. 19, noon to 1 p.m., at 76 Bellevue Avenue in Newport, in person and on Zoom.

The Newport Art Museum's Museum Reads book group meets Thursday, Nov. 19, at noon, with a discussion of Sue Prideaux's biography Wild Thing: A Life of Paul Gauguin. Readers can join in person at the museum at 76 Bellevue Avenue in Newport or by Zoom.

The book

Prideaux's biography re-examines Gauguin's life and work. It follows him from his years as a stockbroker, through a stretch of odd jobs abroad, to the later career in Tahiti that shaped his reputation as a painter.

Who it is for

Museum Reads is an art-themed adult book group that meets monthly on the third Thursday at noon, in person and virtually. It suits readers who want to talk about art history through a single book, whether or not they have read about Gauguin before.

How to join

Members of the museum attend at no charge. Non-members pay $5 per person. Register online to receive email updates and the Zoom link. For a last-minute spot, call the front desk at 401-848-8200 for the Zoom link.
