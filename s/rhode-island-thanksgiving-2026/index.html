Thanksgiving in Rhode Island: The 2026 Guide

Turkey trots, community meals, places to volunteer, Thanksgiving Masses and about 30 restaurants serving dinner on Thursday, Nov. 26.

Thanksgiving in Rhode Island is Thursday, Nov. 26, and the day has room for every kind of tradition: a morning turkey trot, a community table for anyone who wants company, a shift serving meals, a Thanksgiving Mass or a dinner someone else cooks. This guide gathers what organizers have posted so far, from the biggest buffets to the smallest parish service.

Many places haven't posted their 2026 plans yet. Where a listing shows an earlier year's time, we say so. Call ahead before you go, and book restaurant tables early: some are already sold out.

Turkey trots and Thanksgiving morning runs

The Rhode Island Family Turkey Trot starts at the Blackstone Valley Visitor's Center on Roosevelt Avenue in Pawtucket. The children's race begins at 9:30 a.m. and the 5K run/walk at 10 a.m. Entry is listed at $38 for the 5K and $18 for children 12 and younger, and online registration is listed as closing Nov. 25 at 8 p.m. The race benefits the Pawtucket Soup Kitchen, and runners can bring nonperishable food.

The Newport County YMCA's 41st Annual Thanksgiving Day Pie Run starts at 8:30 a.m. at 792 Valley Road in Middletown, with a five-mile race and a three-mile run/walk. Entry is $41 and $35 before a Nov. 20 price increase, plus registration fees. It supports YMCA assistance programs and collects food for the MLK Community Center.

In Providence, CORE Cycle.Fitness.Lagree hosts CORE + Giving at 229 Waterman St.: an outdoor boot camp from 7:30 to 8:15 a.m., then a 5K run/walk from 8:30 to 9:30 a.m. supporting oncology research at Rhode Island Hospital. Either event is $50, or both for $75, with lower prices for ages 4 to 12. Membership isn't needed.

The Bristol County Lions Club's East Bay Turkey Trot is at Colt State Park in Bristol, with families and walkers welcome. Patch lists 7 a.m., while the registration page lists 8 to 10 a.m., so confirm the start time, distance and entry price with the organizer.

Community Thanksgiving meals

These tables are open to anyone who wants a meal or company on Thanksgiving. Several haven't posted 2026 details yet, so call first.

FeedRI's 3rd Annual Friendsgiving, a pop-up Thanksgiving lunch, runs 11 a.m. to 3 p.m. at Farm Fresh RI in Providence, with take-home bags of winter accessories and snacks, all at no charge. FeedRI's announcement lists two different street addresses, so confirm the entrance before you travel.

The WARM Center's Anita's Kitchen, 54 Spruce St., Westerly, serves meals every day of the year, including holidays, with lunch from noon to 1 p.m. and dinner from 5:30 to 6:30 p.m., at no charge and without a referral. Check whether Thanksgiving has a special menu or holiday hours.

Seamen's Church Institute, 18 Market Square, Newport, holds a Thanksgiving Day dinner open to the public at no charge. The 2026 time isn't posted; call 401-847-4260.

McAuley House, 622 Elmwood Ave., Providence, lists Thanksgiving gatherings among its hospitality programs for people seeking food and company. Call 401-941-9013 for 2026 details.

The Pawtucket Soup Kitchen serves a celebratory meal on every holiday from the basement of St. Joseph's Church, entering near the rear parking lot. The 2026 time isn't posted; call 401-258-4616.

Christ Episcopal Church in Westerly has hosted a community Thanksgiving dinner with dining and takeout, served from noon to 2 p.m. in 2023, according to the Westerly Rotary. Sandywoods Center for the Arts, 43 Muse Way, Tiverton, has held a Thanksgiving dinner at no charge with a vegetarian option and an open invitation to musicians; the most recent announcement found is from 2015. Bishop Hendricken High School and Iggy's Boardwalk in Warwick have partnered on a Thanksgiving gathering for people who would otherwise be alone. Check with each organizer that these continue in 2026.

Ways to volunteer and give

Most Thanksgiving volunteer shifts fill early, and none of these organizers has posted whether its spots are full. Sign up ahead and get a confirmed assignment before you show up.

The Newport County YMCA posts Pie Run volunteer jobs on RunSignup, including registration and greeting from 6:15 a.m., course direction and water stations from 6:30 to 10 a.m., and food setup from 8:35 to 10 a.m. Registration volunteers must be 18 or older. Openings showed when checked.

The WARM Center in Westerly asks volunteers to submit its Volunteer Intake Form before seeking a Thanksgiving assignment. The Pawtucket Soup Kitchen asks helpers to call 401-258-4616 ahead, and volunteers 18 and older need a current background check. McAuley House takes help with food preparation and serving; use its volunteer form or call 401-941-9013, ext. 302. FeedRI takes volunteer inquiries for Friendsgiving through its volunteer page, but signing up isn't a confirmed shift.

Christ Episcopal Church in Westerly has used Thanksgiving volunteers to serve, pack takeout, deliver meals and donate cooked turkeys or pies; contact the church first. You can also drop nonperishable food at the Rhode Island Family Turkey Trot's registration area in Pawtucket on race morning for the Pawtucket Soup Kitchen. The page doesn't say whether you must run, so confirm with the organizer.

Take-home Thanksgiving dinners

Waterman Grille, 4 Richmond Square, Providence, offers a cold turkey dinner for four at $225 plus tax, with reheating instructions, for pickup from 11 a.m. to 2 p.m. Order online by Nov. 18; the dining room is closed that day.

The Café, 3 Canal St., Westerly, offers an individual turkey dinner for $60, with extra sides and desserts, for pickup from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. Order online or call 401-357-0300. Its homepage lists a Nov. 23 deadline at 5 p.m., but another page differs, so confirm when ordering.
