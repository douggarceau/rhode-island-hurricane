Rhode Island Comic Con Takes Over Providence, Nov. 6–8

Friday through Sunday at the Rhode Island Convention Center and Amica Mutual Pavilion, with the Sunday session starting at 10 a.m.

Rhode Island Comic Con is the biggest crowd on this weekend's calendar, and it runs this weekend, Friday through Sunday. The three-day show takes place Friday, Nov. 6, through Sunday, Nov. 8, across the Rhode Island Convention Center and Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence.

Three days of pop culture

A Comic Con weekend brings together artists, collectors, costume makers and fans of comics, film, television and games. Visitors can browse the exhibitor tables, see the costumes and follow the schedule of panels and special sessions.

Sunday at Amica Mutual Pavilion

The Sunday session is listed to begin at 10 a.m. at Amica Mutual Pavilion, One LaSalle Square in Providence. No closing time is listed, so plan for a full morning and afternoon.

Getting in

Tickets are sold through the official Rhode Island Comic Con site at riconvention.com. Availability varies by day, and the Sunday listing offers a notification option, so check the event page before you go.
