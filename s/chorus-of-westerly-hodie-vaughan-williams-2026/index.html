Chorus of Westerly Rings In the Season With Vaughan Williams' Hodie

Saturday, November 14 at 6 p.m. and Sunday, November 15 at 3 p.m. at George Kent Performance Hall in Westerly.

The Chorus of Westerly opens its 68th season in mid-November with Ralph Vaughan Williams' Hodie (This Day), a choral celebration of Christmas joy. Two performances are set for the George Kent Performance Hall in Westerly: Saturday, November 14 at 6 p.m., and Sunday, November 15 at 3 p.m.

Vaughan Williams was one of the most admired English composers of the twentieth century, and Hodie is his Christmas cantata. Its sweeping choral writing and bright festive moments make it a natural centerpiece for a concert meant to ring in the season. The work is a celebration rather than a solemn observance, which suits an evening that families and longtime listeners can share. Because the piece draws on the English tradition of carols and festive choral writing, it rewards a chorus that sings with both energy and care, and a listener who comes for the familiar seasonal sound will hear it in fresh ways.

The season ahead

The Chorus of Westerly is in its 68th season, and its calendar runs from this November through a summer concert in Wilcox Park in June. The season also includes a Christmas Pops program on December 19 and 20, a Lyyra concert at Kent Hall in February and a Dvořák Requiem in May.

Getting there and tickets

George Kent Performance Hall is at 119 High Street in Westerly, RI 02891. Individual tickets are sold online around the clock and can be printed at home, picked up at will call or mailed. The Kent Hall box office is open most weekdays from 11 a.m. to 5 p.m. and opens three hours before performances on concert weekends. Cash, checks, Visa, Mastercard and American Express are accepted.
