Country Hall of Famer Marty Stuart Plays the Park Theatre Nov. 8

Marty Stuart and His Fabulous Superlatives bring their instrumental album 'Space Junk' to Cranston on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 7 p.m.

A genuine country legend is headed to Cranston. Marty Stuart and His Fabulous Superlatives play the Historic Park Theatre, 848 Park Avenue, on Sunday, Nov. 8 at 7 p.m.

Who he is

Stuart is a five-time Grammy winner and a member of the Country Music Hall of Fame, with a career that stretches more than 50 years and dozens of albums. He founded the Congress of Country Music and has received the Americana Music Association's Lifetime Achievement Award.

The show

This tour is built around Space Junk, the band's first full-length instrumental album. The Superlatives are Kenny Vaughan, Harry Stinson and Chris Scruggs.

Tickets

Tickets start at $58 through the theater's Etix page at theparkri.com, and all sales are final. A limited number of VIP add-ons include a private pre-show event with a two-song performance and a storytelling Q&A, a laminate, early merchandise shopping and a special-edition poster.
