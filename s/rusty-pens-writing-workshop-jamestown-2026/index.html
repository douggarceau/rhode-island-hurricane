Rusty Pens: A Weekly Writing Workshop for Jamestown Beginners

Mondays from Nov. 9 through Feb. 22, 10:30 to 11:30 a.m., in the Sydney Wright Room of the Jamestown Philomenian Library, 26 North Road, Jamestown.

Some of the best writing starts with a memory. Rusty Pens is a creative writing group that meets at the Jamestown Philomenian Library on Mondays, beginning November 9, from 10:30 to 11:30 a.m. in the Sydney Wright Room.

What happens in the room

Members write and share personal memories and life experiences, reflect on their own stories and trade ideas with one another. The library describes the group as a place for personal stories and creative writing, and it welcomes beginners as well as people who simply want to write for the pleasure of it.

The schedule

The group meets every Monday through February 22, 2027, and the calendar runs straight through the holiday weeks. The first sessions fall on November 9, 16, 23 and 30, and the group continues weekly through December, January and February.

Good to know

The library lists the group for adults. The listing does not give a cost or name a leader, so call the library or check its calendar before the first session. Writers who want to test the water can come once and see whether the room suits them.
