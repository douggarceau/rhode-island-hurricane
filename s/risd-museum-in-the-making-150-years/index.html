RISD Museum opens a 150-year look at how its collection grew

Opens Saturday, Nov. 21 at the RISD Museum, 20 North Main Street in Providence, and stays on view through Aug. 1, 2027.

The RISD Museum is marking the 150th anniversary of the Rhode Island School of Design with a show that asks how the objects in its collection arrived there. In the Making: 150 Years of Collecting opens Saturday, Nov. 21, and runs through Aug. 1, 2027.

The museum now holds more than 100,000 works. The exhibition brings more than 50 of them into view, chosen to show why and how each one entered the collection rather than to tell a single story of art history. The range is wide: one-of-a-kind artworks sit beside large-scale manufactured goods, global textiles and decorative objects, and photographs and posters. Some acquisitions challenged the cultural norms of their day, and the show looks closely at those choices.

Behind the collection

Emily Banas, associate curator of decorative arts and design, is the curator. The premise is that a museum collection is never finished. Each object came through a gift, a purchase or a decision by someone who thought it mattered, and walking the galleries lays those decisions side by side.

Planning a visit

The exhibition page does not list related programs or an admission price, so check the museum's hours and admission page before you go. The museum is at 20 North Main Street in Providence.
