Dave Matthews Band Brings a Big Monday Night to Amica Mutual Pavilion

Monday, Nov. 9 at Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence; check the venue listing for the start time and doors.

Dave Matthews Band is in Providence tonight, Monday, Nov. 9, for a show at Amica Mutual Pavilion, the arena formerly known as the Dunkin' Donuts Center. It is the fourth stop on a seven-date fall run that opened Nov. 4 at the Kohl Center in Madison, Wis. The run closes with two nights at Madison Square Garden on Nov. 12 and 13.

Tickets and timing

Presale access went first to members of the band's Warehouse Fan Association. General sales opened Aug. 21 at 10 a.m. local time. The coverage we read did not list ticket prices, the show's start time or the doors time, so check the venue's listing for those before you leave, and allow extra time for parking and entry on a busy weeknight.

A summer surprise and a long run

Matthews made an unannounced appearance at the Newport Folk Festival on July 26, joining Nathaniel Rateliff & Friends during the closing set on the Fort Stage. Joan Baez and Mavis Staples were also surprise guests that day. The band resumes its summer tour on Aug. 28 and wraps with its traditional Labor Day weekend run at the Gorge Amphitheatre in Washington. Since its debut in 1991, the band has sold more than 25 million tickets, according to the report.

Good neighbors on the road

The band has worked with the environmental nonprofit REVERB for 21 years. Its reforestation support for The Nature Conservancy is expected to reach 7 million trees planted by the end of 2026, the band says.

For a night out in one of the region's larger arenas, arrive with time to spare, check the venue's bag and entry rules, and enjoy an evening of live music in the heart of Providence.
