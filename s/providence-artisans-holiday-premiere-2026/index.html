Holiday Premiere: Handmade Gifts at WaterFire Arts Center, Nov. 15

Sunday, Nov. 15 from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. at 475 Valley Street in Providence, with no-charge admission.

Holiday shopping in Rhode Island has a new starting point this month. On Sunday, Nov. 15, the 2026 Providence Artisans Holiday Premiere fills the WaterFire Arts Center at 475 Valley Street in Providence with local makers from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m.

What you will find

The show brings together local artists who display and sell their own handmade work. Shoppers can expect jewelry, paintings, ceramics, glass, clothing, accessories and mixed-media pieces, which makes it a good place to look for gifts with a personal touch.

Coffee and food trucks will be on site, so a visit can stretch into a relaxed outing. Admission is no charge, and parking is no charge as well.

Why it is worth going

Craft fairs are a chance to meet the people who make the things they sell. Ask a potter about a glaze or a jeweler about a stone, and the conversation often becomes the best part of the day. With an event open to everyone at no cost, it is an easy way to bring a friend, a child or a neighbor along.

Plan your visit

Doors open at 10:00 a.m. and the premiere closes at 4:00 p.m. on Sunday, Nov. 15. The address is 475 Valley Street in Providence, the WaterFire Arts Center, where the event is held.
