This Weekend in Rhode Island: Comic Con, The Great Gatsby and WaterFire for Veterans

Comic Con fills the Convention Center, The Great Gatsby plays PPAC, WaterFire honors veterans Saturday night, and church bazaars open the holiday shopping season, Nov. 6 to 8.

The first full weekend of November brings Comic Con to downtown Providence, a Broadway tour at PPAC and the season's first big run of church holiday bazaars. Here are the weekend's top events, Friday through Sunday.

WaterFire Salute to Veterans

WaterFire lights the downtown Providence riverfront Saturday for its Salute to Veterans. Activities start at 3:30 p.m., and the fires burn from 4:32 to 10 p.m.

Rhode Island Comic Con

Rhode Island Comic Con runs all three days, Friday through Sunday, at the Rhode Island Convention Center and Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence. Check ricomiccon.com for hours and tickets.

Outdoors and family

The Providence Winter Farmers Market opens for the season Saturday, 9 a.m. to 1 p.m., at Farm Fresh RI, 10 Sims Ave. Frosty Drew Observatory in Charlestown canceled its Friday stargazing night because of heavy rain and overcast skies. At Blithewold in Bristol, Marvelous Maples with Joe Verstandig is Saturday, 11 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. Authors Cassandra Clare and Holly Black appear together Sunday at 6 p.m. at the RISD Auditorium in Providence.
