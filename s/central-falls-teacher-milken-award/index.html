Central Falls Teacher Briana Jones Surprised With $25,000 Milken Award

She thought she was cooking for visiting dignitaries. Instead she got the award often called the Oscar of teaching.

Briana Jones thought she was preparing food for state education officials visiting the new Central Falls High School. The visit was really for her.

Jones, a special education and culinary teacher, was surprised with a 2026 Milken Educator Award, which comes with $25,000 and is often called the Oscar of teaching. She's the first recipient on this year's national tour, which honors 30 educators out of about 3.7 million teachers across the country.

Her work

Jones has taught for 15 years. She works with students ages 18 to 22 in the school's transition program, helping them build skills for life after graduation. She recently took on a leadership role in the culinary career and technical education program, which runs a student-staffed cafe at the school.

Dr. Jane Foley, senior vice president of the Milken Educator Awards, made the announcement. Jones says she plans to spend most of the money on her students.
