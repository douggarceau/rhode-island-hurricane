Contemporary Theater Company Brings A Christmas Carol to Wakefield

Performances run on select dates through Dec. 20 at Contemporary Theater Company in Wakefield, with Sunday matinees at 2 p.m. and evening curtains at 7 p.m.

Few stories have held a place on Rhode Island stages as reliably as Charles Dickens's A Christmas Carol. This December, Contemporary Theater Company in Wakefield offers its own staging of the tale of Ebenezer Scrooge, with performances on select dates through December 20.

Dickens published the novella in 1843. Its story of a miserly man visited on Christmas Eve by three spirits has since been adapted for theatre, film and radio, and it remains a fixture of the holiday stage.

The company has staged the show in earlier seasons. BroadwayWorld covered its production in 2018 and again in 2024, when the company announced its holiday presentation.

Dates and times

Ticket listings show performances on Friday, Dec. 4 at 7 p.m.; Sunday, Dec. 6 at 2 p.m.; Thursday, Dec. 10 at 7 p.m.; Friday, Dec. 11 at 7 p.m.; Sunday, Dec. 13 at 2 p.m.; and Sunday, Dec. 20 at 2 p.m. Schedules can change, so check the company's season page before you plan.

Getting tickets

Tickets are sold through the company and its ticketing partners. The Sunday afternoon curtains suit families, while the Friday evening performances make an easy start to a holiday weekend.

A December matinee also pairs well with the area's other seasonal offerings. The Vienna Boys Choir performs at the Park Theatre in Cranston on Dec. 5 at 7:30 p.m., so a theatre outing and a choir concert can share a single holiday weekend.
