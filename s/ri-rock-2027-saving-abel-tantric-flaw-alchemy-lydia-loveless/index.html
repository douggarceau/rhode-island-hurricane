Post-Grunge in a 300-Cap Room: Saving Abel, Tantric and Flaw Hit Alchemy

Three radio-rock road dogs share a Sunday night bill on Chestnut Street on Jan. 24, leading a 2027 calendar that runs from Woonsocket to Lincoln.

The holiday shows are still rolling, but the smart money is already circling dates in the new year. The loudest early entry on Rhode Island's 2027 calendar is a triple bill of 2000s radio rock: Saving Abel, Tantric and Flaw, together at Alchemy in Providence on Sunday, Jan. 24, according to tour listings on Concerts50 and Songkick.

The show

Concerts50 lists the Providence date as a 5 p.m. Sunday show at Alchemy with all three bands on the bill. Songkick's venue page puts Alchemy at 171 Chestnut St. in Providence and lists its capacity at 300. That is the hook here: bands built for rock radio and bigger stages, packed into a room where the back wall is never far from the front of the stage.

An early Sunday start also makes this an easy school-night show. Door times, set order and prices were not posted on the listings reviewed for this story, so check alchemyri.net before you buy.

Who is on the bill

The Jan. 24 night is part of a short Northeast run. Concerts50 lists Saving Abel and Tantric at Off The Rails Music Venue in Worcester, Mass., the night before, on Saturday, Jan. 23, and then all three bands at Jergel's Rhythm Grille in Warrendale, Pa., on Wednesday, Jan. 27.

If you miss Providence, the Worcester date is the closest backup, though Concerts50 lists only Saving Abel and Tantric for that night.

The rest of the early 2027 board

Alchemy is not the only room booking ahead. Songkick's Providence listings show a run of winter and spring dates across the state, from the downtown club scene to theaters and the casino stage in Lincoln.

A few of these stand out. Lydia Loveless brings her show to the same small Chestnut Street room in February. The Lovin' Spoonful land at the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket at the end of that month, and Roger McGuinn plays the Greenwich Odeum in East Greenwich in April. And for anyone who misses the hair-metal era, Slaughter is on the books at Bally's in Lincoln in May.

Why it matters

Rhode Island's club scene lives on nights like this. A 300-capacity club with a three-band bill keeps Providence on the touring map for rock bands, and puts fans close enough to see the strings move.

The January show also lands in the quiet stretch after New Year's, when the holiday concerts are over and the summer festivals are months away. A Sunday triple bill is a good way to break up a cold month.

How to go

Alchemy is at 171 Chestnut St. in Providence. The Jan. 24 show starts at 5 p.m., per Concerts50. Ticket links for each date are on the venue and Songkick listings; confirm times and prices before you head out, since listings can change.
