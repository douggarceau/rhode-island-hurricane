WaterFire's Salute to Veterans Lights Up Providence Nov. 7

150 veterans carry torches into WaterPlace Park for the 14th annual tribute, with a veterans resource fair and a ceremony at the State House.

One of the most moving nights on the Providence river comes back on Saturday, Nov. 7. WaterFire Providence holds its 14th annual Salute to Veterans, a lighting dedicated to the men and women who served.

What happens

The night begins with opening ceremonies on the State House steps, and the public is invited. Then about 150 veterans carrying torches process into the WaterPlace Park basin and form a ring of fire as the braziers on the river are lit. Veterans and active-duty service members are invited to serve as torchbearers, with stationary spots for anyone who needs help getting around.

The resource fair

A Veterans Resource Fair, led by the Providence VA Medical Center, runs alongside the lighting. In past years it has run through the afternoon and evening, with Taps played in Memorial Park around 6:35 p.m. WaterFire's event page lists onshore programming from 3:30 p.m., the speaking program at 4 p.m., the boat procession at 4:31 p.m., the fires lit beginning at 4:49 p.m., the Veterans torch ceremony at 4:50 p.m., and the fires burning until 10 p.m. It does not list a Taps time for this year, so check waterfire.org before you head out.

Where to watch

Good viewing spots include the WaterPlace Park basin, Memorial Park and the Crawford Street and Washington Street bridges. WaterFire can be called off for weather, so check the day of.

Veterans who want to carry a torch can contact WaterFire through its website.
