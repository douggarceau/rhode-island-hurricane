NaBSCO Community Orchestra Opens Its Season in East Providence

Sunday, Nov. 8 at 3 p.m. in the East Providence High School auditorium, 2000 Pawtucket Avenue, with John Eells conducting and pay-what-you-can admission.

The Narragansett Bay Symphony Community Orchestra, known as NaBSCO, opens its season this Sunday at 3 p.m. in the auditorium at East Providence High School, 2000 Pawtucket Avenue. John Eells conducts the season-opening concert, and admission is pay what you can.

NaBSCO is a community orchestra, a group of volunteer musicians who rehearse through the fall and perform for the public. A season opener gives audiences a first look at the year's programs and a chance to hear local players in a school auditorium.

The 2024 season opener, led by guest conductor Zeke Fetrow, included Tchaikovsky's Marche Slave, Ravel's Le Tombeau de Couperin and Florence Price's Symphony No. 3 in C minor. Price's symphony is a welcome addition to any orchestral program, and it is the kind of piece a community orchestra can bring to its audience.

Guests at that concert were asked to pay what they thought was fair and to bring a non-perishable food item for the Rhode Island Community Food Bank. Bring a can or a box if you can; the orchestra has asked for food donations before.

Details

Sunday, Nov. 8, 3 p.m., East Providence High School auditorium, 2000 Pawtucket Avenue, East Providence, RI 02914. Admission is pay what you can. Learn more at nabsco.org, email team@nabsco.org, or call 401-274-4578.
