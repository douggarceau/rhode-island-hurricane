November's Big Shows: Dave Matthews, Jeff Tweedy, Marty Stuart and More

From an arena night at the AMP to a Wilco frontman at the Uptown, here are the concerts worth circling this month.

November is stacked. Rhode Island gets an arena headliner, a run of singer-songwriters in smaller rooms, a country legend, a Grateful Dead night and a full orchestra, all before Thanksgiving. Here are the shows to know about.

The big one: Dave Matthews Band, Nov. 9

Dave Matthews Band plays the Amica Mutual Pavilion in Providence on Monday, Nov. 9. It's the fourth stop on a short seven-city fall run that opens Nov. 4 in Madison, Wis., and wraps with two nights at Madison Square Garden. Rhode Island got a preview this summer: Matthews walked on unannounced at the Newport Folk Festival on July 26, sat in with Nathaniel Rateliff & Friends alongside Joan Baez and Mavis Staples, and opened with "Flowers."

Jeff Tweedy at the Uptown, Nov. 3

The Wilco frontman brings his solo tour, with Case Oats, to the Uptown Theater in Providence on Tuesday, Nov. 3 at 7:30 p.m.

Cranston's Park Theatre doubles up

The Historic Park Theatre in Cranston has two very different nights: pop singer Andy Grammer on Friday, Nov. 6, and country great Marty Stuart and His Fabulous Superlatives on Sunday, Nov. 8.

Friday, Nov. 6 is a big night

Besides Andy Grammer, '70s hitmakers Orleans play the Greenwich Odeum in East Greenwich, and Playing Dead, one of the region's favorite Grateful Dead bands, is on the calendar at The Met in Pawtucket. Over in Woonsocket, Jay Leno brings his comedy to the Stadium Theatre at 8 p.m., with tickets listed at $86 to $136.

The orchestra and the arena

The Rhode Island Philharmonic plays "Ride of the Valkyries!" at The VETS in Providence on Saturday, Nov. 21. And on Saturday, Nov. 28, reggaeton star Nicky Jam brings his Perreoland show to the Amica Mutual Pavilion at 8 p.m.

Every night of the week

These are the headliners. The Hurricane's calendar lists the club shows, tribute nights, folk concerts and open mics happening in between, from AS220 and Fête to The Met and Blackstone River Theatre. Dates and times can change, so check with the venue before you go.
