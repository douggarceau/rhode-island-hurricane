Cirque du Soleil's 'Twas the Night Before Plays PPAC Through Dec. 6

The holiday show runs Tuesday, Nov. 24, through Sunday, Dec. 6, at PPAC, 220 Weybosset Street, Providence.

Cirque du Soleil's holiday production 'Twas the Night Before… comes to PPAC for a run from Tuesday, Nov. 24, through Sunday, Dec. 6, 2026. It is part of PPAC's Encore Series, announced alongside the venue's 2026-27 Broadway season.

The show does not play every day in that stretch. PPAC's schedule has no performances on Thursday, Nov. 26, Monday, Nov. 30, or Tuesday, Dec. 1, so check the calendar before you plan a visit.

Family night and tickets

BankNewport Family Night is Wednesday, Dec. 2, at 7 p.m. Showtimes are listed on PPAC's website, which also has the venue's address at 220 Weybosset Street in downtown Providence. Individual on-sale dates had not been announced when PPAC published its season, but groups of 10 or more can book now through the box office at 401.421.ARTS (2787).

Arriving early gives you time to find parking downtown before the house lights go down.
