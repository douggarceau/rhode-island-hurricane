Classical Christmas With Frisson Plays Emmanuel Church in Newport

Saturday, Dec. 12 at 1:00 and 3:30 p.m., Emmanuel Church, 42 Dearborn St., Newport.

Newport Classical brings its holiday season to Emmanuel Church on Saturday, December 12, 2026, with two performances of Classical Christmas with Frisson, at 1:00 p.m. and 3:30 p.m. The church, at 42 Dearborn Street in Newport, is an English Gothic Revival building, and its setting suits a program of this kind.

Frisson is an 11-piece chamber orchestra that describes itself as the classical group that does it all. The program is subject to change, but the planned selections pair baroque favorites with seasonal music.

The program

The announced repertoire opens with the Sinfonia from Handel's Messiah and includes the Adagio movement of Bach's Concerto for violin and oboe, BWV 1060, and Bach's Brandenburg Concerto No. 4. Winter from Vivaldi's The Four Seasons represents the Baroque, while Tchaikovsky's Nutcracker Suite and Frackenpohl's A Christmas Jazz Suite bring the holiday spirit into the program. The concert also includes a traditional Christmas sing-along and a version of "Carol of the Bells" arranged by the ensemble.

The format suits newcomers as well as regular concertgoers. An ensemble of eleven players in an intimate church, moving from baroque concertos to a sing-along, gives the afternoon a warm, communal feel, and the two performance times make it easy to fit into a December day in Newport.

Tickets and details

Tickets are $35 for main seating and $55 for premier seating. The ticket link on the event page covers the 3:30 p.m. performance, and details for the 1:00 p.m. concert are available on the Newport Classical website. Newport Classical presents the concerts at Emmanuel Church, 42 Dearborn Street, Newport, RI 02840.
