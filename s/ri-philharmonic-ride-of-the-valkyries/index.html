RI Philharmonic Plays 'Ride of the Valkyries!' at The VETS

Wagner, Clara Schumann's Piano Concerto, Dvorak and Ravel's La Valse, Saturday, Nov. 21 at 7:30 p.m. Tickets start at $25.

The Rhode Island Philharmonic Orchestra has a big night planned this month. "Ride of the Valkyries!" takes the stage at The VETS, 1 Avenue of the Arts in Providence, on Saturday, Nov. 21 at 7:30 p.m. Doors open at 6:30.

The program

Christian Reif conducts Wagner's Ride of the Valkyries, Clara Schumann's Piano Concerto with pianist Estefan Iatcekiw, Dvorak's The Wood Dove and Ravel's swirling La Valse.

Tickets

Tickets start at $25 and are sold only through the Philharmonic at tickets.riphil.org or its box office at 401-248-7000. The VETS box office can't sell them.
