Environmental Currents: Art and Ocean Science at URI's Studio Blue

Open Monday through Friday, 9 a.m. to 5 p.m., at Studio Blue on URI's Narragansett Bay Campus in Narragansett, through Nov. 18, with no charge admission.

Artists and ocean scientists have worked side by side for Environmental Currents: Art and Science Explore Our Changing World, on view in Studio Blue, the gallery in the Coastal Institute Building on the University of Rhode Island's Narragansett Bay Campus. The exhibition runs through November 18 and is presented by Art League RI and the university.

It is the fourth group show from The Synergy Project, which has paired artists and scientists since 2020. Each work interprets oceanographic research from URI's Graduate School of Oceanography, its College of the Environment and Life Sciences and the Woods Hole Oceanographic Institution. The goal is to present ocean science in an unconventional way that sparks conversation about climate regulation and ocean health.

The pairings

Each piece is credited to a team of one artist and one researcher. The titles include Ocean Tunnels, Blue Atlas, Ancient Air, Overturn, Denizens and Resilience: Study of the Leopard Seal, and the group also includes works titled Chameleon, Pulex and Rubrum. Some of the teams come from the university and some from Woods Hole, so visitors see the same research approached from a painter's, sculptor's or printmaker's side.

Visiting

Studio Blue is at 218 South Ferry Road in Narragansett and is open Monday through Friday from 9 a.m. to 5 p.m. Admission is no charge. The exhibition opened with an artist and scientist reception in September, and special programming was held on September 26 during Science Saturday, when the university's oceanographers opened their doors to the public. A second Synergy Project exhibition is set to open at Studio Blue in late November.
