Peace Dale Congregational Church Holds Its 63rd Christmas Bazaar

Saturday, Dec. 5, 9 a.m. to 2 p.m., at Peace Dale Congregational Church on Columbia Street in Peace Dale, Wakefield.

Peace Dale Congregational Church holds its 63rd annual Christmas bazaar on Saturday, Dec. 5, at 261 Columbia Street in Peace Dale, part of Wakefield in South County. The holiday fundraiser supports the church's local missions and programs, and doors open at 9 a.m. and close at 2 p.m.

Shopping and Crafts

The large white elephant sale fills a room with toys, games, kitchenware, decor, small appliances and winter sports gear. A collectibles area features antique dolls, and a separate room holds vintage jewelry. Local vendors sell artwork, air plants, 3D-printed items, crocheted goods and handmade jewelry, alongside fresh wreaths and holiday greenery. Tabitha's Closet offers gently used clothing and accessories.

Food and Raffle

Shoppers can buy baked goods and homemade jams, join a cookie stroll and sit down to a lunch of homemade soups. Take-home containers are available for anyone who wants to bring the meal home. A raffle offers themed baskets filled with local goods.

A Morning for Younger Visitors

A Christmas village and a model train display give children something to watch and do between stops. Admission carries no charge. Parking is available at the church, along one side of Columbia Street and at South Kingstown High School. The listing does not mention music, so this bazaar is best for browsing, treats and a morning out in South County.
