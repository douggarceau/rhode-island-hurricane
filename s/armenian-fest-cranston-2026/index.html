Armenian Fest Returns to Rhodes on the Pawtuxet in Cranston

Saturday, Nov. 14, noon to 8 p.m., and Sunday, Nov. 15, noon to 7 p.m., at Sts. Vartanantz Armenian Apostolic Church's Rhodes on the Pawtuxet, 60 Rhodes Place, Cranston.

For its 61st year, the Armenian Fest RI fills the grounds of Sts. Vartanantz Armenian Apostolic Church at Rhodes on the Pawtuxet in Cranston. The festival opens Saturday, November 14, from noon to 8 p.m. and continues Sunday, November 15, from noon to 7 p.m., at 60 Rhodes Place.

The festival began in 1965 as a small church bazaar. The parish describes its purpose as bringing people together to celebrate Armenian culture, and it welcomes the wider Rhode Island community to take part.

What to eat

Parishioners make every dish from scratch. The menu includes shish, losh and kufteh dinners, chicken dinners, sandwiches and mezze, and the bakery offers choreg, baklava, bourma and khadayif. The parish says 2026 prices will be posted closer to the festival.

Music, dance and more

Live bands play traditional and contemporary Armenian music across the weekend. Traditional dance performances are scheduled, and visitors are invited to join in the line dancing. There are activities for kids, a raffle table and vendors selling Armenian gifts and cultural goods.

Getting there

Admission is no charge, and all ages are welcome. On-site parking is also at no charge, and the church has arranged valet service through United Parking.
