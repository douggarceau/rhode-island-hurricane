An evening on abstract art with Sheila Isham's work in Newport

Wednesday, Nov. 18 at 5:30 p.m. at the Newport Art Museum, 76 Bellevue Avenue in Newport, with no charge but limited tickets to reserve online.

The Newport Art Museum is hosting an evening built around Sheila Isham: Between Worlds, the exhibition now on view in its galleries. The program, called Experiencing the Abstract: Ways of Seeing, begins Wednesday, Nov. 18, at 5:30 p.m.

Artistic Director Danielle Ogden opens the evening with an overview of Isham's life and practice. Then Luned Palmer, a New York City-based museum educator, leads close-looking exercises. Guests study the artworks, note the associations and reactions they bring with them, and consider how personal responses shape the way anyone understands abstract art.

Reservations

Admission is no charge, but the museum says tickets are limited and recommends booking ahead. Reservations are made through the museum's website. Choose a quantity, add the reservation to your cart and complete the order there.

Getting there

The museum is at 76 Bellevue Avenue in Newport. Arrive a few minutes early so you are settled before the program begins at 5:30 p.m.

Why it suits a fall evening

The format is conversational rather than a lecture. Visitors who have never thought much about abstraction get a guided way in, and regular museum-goers get a fresh look at a living artist's work in the company of an outside educator. For a mid-November week, it is a quiet, thoughtful way to spend an hour and a half.
