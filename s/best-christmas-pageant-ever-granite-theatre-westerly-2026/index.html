The Best Christmas Pageant Ever opens at Westerly's Granite Theatre

Daily from Friday, Nov. 27 through Sunday, Dec. 20 at the Granite Theatre, 1 Granite Street, Westerly; check showtimes with the theatre.

The Best Christmas Pageant Ever opens its holiday run at the Granite Theatre in Westerly on Friday, Nov. 27, and continues daily through Sunday, Dec. 20. The theatre's listing describes it as a delightful show for the whole family, which makes it a natural choice for a holiday outing.

The Story

The production follows the Herdman kids, described on the listing as the most mischievous children in town. When they take over the annual Christmas pageant, the show moves through a run of unexpected twists and plenty of humor. Underneath the laughs is a message about the true spirit of Christmas.

Know Before You Go

The listing does not give performance times or a ticket price, so check before you head out. The Granite Theatre is at 1 Granite Street in Westerly, and the theatre's phone line is (401) 596-2341. The listing also points to a website for ticket details.
