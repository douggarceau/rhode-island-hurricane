Gamm Stages Arthur Miller's A View From the Bridge This November

This November at the Gamm Theatre in Warwick, with dates and curtain times listed on the theatre's season page.

Arthur Miller's A View From the Bridge is the November production in the Gamm Theatre's Season 42, the 2026–27 subscription lineup at the Sandra Feinstein-Gamm Theatre in Warwick. Miller's tragedy follows a Brooklyn longshoreman, his family and the close-knit waterfront neighborhood around them.

Miller wrote the play in the mid-1950s, and it opened on Broadway in 1955. Its setting is a single household and its streets, and its pressure builds slowly in a small space, which suits an intimate theatre like the Gamm.

The rest of the season

The season around it is ambitious. The Smuggler by Ronan Noone runs in September and October. The English Understand Wool, adapted by Tony Estrella from Helen DeWitt's novella, is a world premiere for January and February 2027. Rosencrantz and Guildenstern Are Dead by Tom Stoppard follows in March and April, and Sonia Flew by Melinda Lopez closes the season in April and May.

The holiday production, It's A Wonderful Life: A Live Radio Play, adapted by Joe Landry, is also part of the season. Its dates are not yet posted on the season page.

Tickets

Season 42 subscriptions and memberships went on sale to the public on May 6, 2026. Subscriptions run from $225 to $325, with discounts for seniors, students and groups of eight or more. Single tickets have been sold on a rolling basis since July 2026. Subscribers and members pay $50 for It's A Wonderful Life, and family packages of four tickets for that show cost $200 for the general public.

For dates, curtain times and single tickets, visit gammtheatre.org/season42 or call 401-723-4266. Plays and dates are subject to change, so check the site before you go.
