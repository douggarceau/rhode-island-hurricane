Fly Tying Nights at Louttit Library in West Greenwich

Thursdays from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. through November and December at Louttit Library, West Greenwich; $5 per person per night.

Every Thursday evening this fall, the Louttit Library in West Greenwich hosts the Annual Fall Fly Tying Series, a hands-on workshop for anglers who want to tie their own flies. The sessions run from 6:30 to 8:30 p.m. through November and December, and our calendar lists one on Thursday, Nov. 19.

The series is run by the Rhode Island Department of Environmental Management's Aquatic Resource Education program, which supplies all of the materials and tools. It is aimed at beginner and intermediate fly tyers, and it covers both freshwater and saltwater patterns, so participants can leave with a few patterns they can use on their next trip.

Who can come and what it costs

The fee is $5 per person for each evening. The series is open to adults, and families are welcome with children ten and older. Because space is limited, the state recommends pre-registering through the form on the program's page, and questions can be sent to the program contact at the Department of Environmental Management.

Louttit Library is a community hub in West Greenwich, and the fly tying nights are a good example of the quiet, practical programs that keep its calendar full through the holiday season.
