The Great Gatsby Musical Roars Into PPAC, Nov. 3–8

The Tony-winning Broadway musical brings the Roaring Twenties to downtown Providence for eight performances.

Broadway's take on F. Scott Fitzgerald's novel comes to the Providence Performing Arts Center for one week only, Tuesday, Nov. 3 through Sunday, Nov. 8.

The show

The Great Gatsby follows Jay Gatsby and his pursuit of Daisy Buchanan through the parties and heartbreak of 1920s Long Island. Music and lyrics are by Jason Howland and Nathan Tysen, with a book by Kait Kerrigan, direction by Marc Bruni and choreography by Dominique Kelley.

The production opened on Broadway in April 2024 and won that year's Tony Award for Best Costume Design for Linda Cho's work. It also took the Broadway.com Audience Choice Award for Favorite New Musical.

Showtimes

Tuesday and Wednesday, Nov. 3–4, at 7 p.m.; Thursday and Friday, Nov. 5–6, at 7:30 p.m.; Saturday, Nov. 7, at 1 and 7:30 p.m.; and Sunday, Nov. 8, at 1 and 6:30 p.m.

Tickets

Buy online at ppacri.org, in person at the PPAC box office at 220 Weybosset St., or by phone at 401-421-ARTS (2787). Groups of 10 or more can call PPAC group sales at 401-574-3162.

Heads up: Rhode Island Comic Con fills downtown the same weekend, Nov. 6–8, so leave extra time for parking.
