Holiday Concerts in the Manor Living Room at Blithewold

Saturdays and Sundays, Dec. 12, 13, 19 and 20, 1:00 to 2:00 PM, in the Living Room in the Manor at Blithewold, Bristol.

Blithewold, the historic estate on Ferry Road in Bristol, is offering a short run of afternoon concerts in December. On four weekend days, the Living Room in the Manor hosts a program that runs about an hour, from 1:00 to 2:00 PM.

The series covers Saturday, Dec. 12, Sunday, Dec. 13, Saturday, Dec. 19 and Sunday, Dec. 20. Mezzo-soprano Nancy Hood performs on the 12th and the 20th. On Sunday, Dec. 13, jazz pianist Laszlo Gardony takes the piano. The performer for Saturday, Dec. 19 has not yet been announced.

A Setting Made for the Season

The concerts take place while the Manor is decorated for the holidays, so listeners hear music in the same rooms that carry the seasonal displays. A single room keeps the audience close to the performers, which suits the small-ensemble and solo format of the series.

Tickets and Planning

Members pay $5 and non-members pay $28. Tickets include admission to the Manor and the grounds, so guests can walk the gardens before or after the concert. Registration is required, so reserve a seat ahead of the date you choose.

Blithewold is at 101 Ferry Road, Route 114, in Bristol. The hour-long format makes the concerts an easy stop between other holiday plans, and the weekend afternoons leave room for a longer visit to the estate.
