Foundry Artists' Holiday Show Brings 65 Artists to Pawtucket

Thursday, Dec. 3 at the Pawtucket Armory Center for the Arts, 172 Exchange Street, Pawtucket, with original art and handmade gifts.

The Foundry Artists Holiday Show returns to the Pawtucket Armory Center for the Arts, 172 Exchange Street in Pawtucket, on Thursday, Dec. 3. The show brings together original art and handmade gifts from 65 artists, giving shoppers a chance to see a wide range of work in one place and find something special for the people on their list.

Original art makes a particularly lasting gift. A piece bought directly from the maker carries a story about how it was made, and a holiday show like this one lets visitors meet many artists in a single visit rather than tracking down each studio separately. For many people, that is the easiest way to start a holiday shopping list with something personal.

Shoppers can browse at their own pace across the work of all 65 artists, comparing styles and prices and choosing gifts that suit a range of budgets. Because the event is a juried gathering of working artists, every table is a chance to support a local creative.

Admission is listed at no charge, so the show is an easy addition to a weekday outing in December. The event page does not list hours, so check with the Pawtucket Armory Center for the Arts before you go.

Holiday art shows are a tradition across Rhode Island, and this one is a good chance to support working artists during the busiest shopping weeks of the year. Plan a visit on Dec. 3 and bring the family to see what they have made.
