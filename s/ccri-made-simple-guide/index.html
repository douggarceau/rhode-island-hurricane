CCRI, Made Simple: Dates, Offices and Campuses in One Place

The Community College of Rhode Island's key dates, offices and campuses, in Rhode Island Hurricane's new CCRI guide.

The Community College of Rhode Island has six campuses and education centers and dozens of offices, and its website can be hard to navigate. Rhode Island Hurricane's CCRI Guide puts the essentials in one view.

Free for everyone

Every campus, upcoming events, the links students use most, and the next three key dates.

Free with a reader account

CCRI's full calendar of 44 dates, from registration and add/drop to withdrawal, finals and commencement, plus direct phone numbers, emails and hours for 19 offices, including One Stop, financial aid, advising and the IT help desk.

The guide is independent and isn't run by CCRI. Check the college's official site for changes.
