The Chorus of Westerly Brings Its Christmas Pops to George Kent Hall

Sunday, Dec. 20, at 5:30 p.m., at The George Kent Performance Hall, 119 High Street in Westerly

The Chorus of Westerly brings its Christmas Pops concert to The George Kent Performance Hall, 119 High Street in Westerly, on Sunday, Dec. 20, at 5:30 p.m. The chorus describes the evening as a sharing of holiday favorites, new and old, and as part of a long-standing holiday tradition in the town.

The program is built for the season. The chorus sings a mix of carols and holiday songs that many families already know, along with some pieces that are newer to the repertoire. The chorus's listing names no soloists or guest performers, so the evening belongs to the chorus itself, singing together in the hall where its concerts are held.

A Westerly Tradition

Christmas Pops is one of the chorus's signature holiday evenings, and it gives audiences a way to begin the season close to home. For residents of Westerly and the neighboring shore towns, it is a concert that fits easily between shopping, dinner and the first lights of December.

Tickets

Tickets are sold through the chorus's online ticket link, which is listed on its Christmas Pops page. The page does not state a ticket price, so check the listing before you go. The Chorus of Westerly is a certified 501(c)(3) charitable organization, and donations to the chorus and its programs are tax-deductible to the fullest extent the law allows.

Those who want a choral concert with a holiday glow will find this one an easy choice. Arrive early for a good seat, and bring a friend who has never heard the chorus sing in person.
