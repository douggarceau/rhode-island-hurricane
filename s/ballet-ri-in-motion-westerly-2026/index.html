Ballet RI's In Motion Comes to Westerly's United Theatre, Nov. 6

Friday, November 6 at 7:30 p.m. at the United Theatre in Westerly, with three works including a company premiere.

Ballet Rhode Island brings its In Motion program to the United Theatre in Westerly on Friday, November 6, at 7:30 p.m. The evening shows the company's range, with a work drawn from American Impressionism alongside a new premiere made with a local educational partner.

What is on the program

The program includes Bathers, choreographed by Yury Yanowsky and inspired by American Impressionism and its attention to light and open air. City of Women, by James Whiteside, is presented as a company premiere, which means Ballet RI is performing the work for the first time.

Pairing a painterly piece with a premiere rooted in history gives the evening a wide emotional arc, moving from light and leisure toward memory and reflection.

The third work is a world premiere created with the Sandra Bornstein Holocaust Education Center. It is a reminder that dance can carry serious history, and a chance to see the company work alongside a local educational center.

Venue and tickets

The performance takes place at the United Theatre, 5 Canal Street in Westerly. Ballet RI does not sell tickets for this date, so buy through the United Theatre. The same program opened the company's season at the Woodman Center at Moses Brown in Providence in October, and this Westerly date brings it to South County. The listed evening runs from 7:00 to 9:00 p.m., so plan for about two hours of dance, including the intermission.

The season ahead

Ballet RI's season continues with The Nutcracker at The VETS in Providence, with performances from December 17 through 27. Ballet Meets The Beatles follows in March at the Woodman Center.
