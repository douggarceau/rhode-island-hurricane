A Live Radio Play of It's a Wonderful Life Comes to Woonsocket

Thursday, Dec. 3 at 7:30 p.m., Saturday, Dec. 5 at 2 and 7:30 p.m., and Sunday, Dec. 6 at 2 p.m. at the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket.

Stage adaptations of the holiday classic come in many shapes, and this one takes the form of a radio broadcast from the 1940s. At the Stadium Theatre in Woonsocket, a cast of a few dozen characters tells the story of George Bailey as if it were going out over the air, presented as a live radio program.

The script is by Joe Landry and draws on Philip Van Doren Stern's story "The Greatest Gift," the source of the 1946 film that many families know by heart.

Performance dates and tickets

Performances are set for Thursday, Dec. 3 at 7:30 p.m., Saturday, Dec. 5 at 2 and 7:30 p.m., and Sunday, Dec. 6 at 2 p.m. Tickets are $26. The theatre is at 28 Monument Square in Woonsocket, and the event listing places the show in its Marquee Room.

The theatre's page notes that tickets bought through third-party websites are not covered for fulfillment or refunds, so buying directly from the box office is the simplest route.

Who it suits

The stage page lists the production as having mature content, so check the listing before bringing younger children. For adults, it offers a familiar story told in a historic downtown theatre during the weeks when many people begin their holiday plans.
