Happy Mail: Jamestown Library Makes Thank-You Postcards on Nov. 14

Saturday, Nov. 14, 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. in the Sydney Wright Room of the Jamestown Philomenian Library, 26 North Road, Jamestown.

Adults in Jamestown can spend part of a Saturday morning writing a thank-you on paper. The Jamestown Philomenian Library's Happy Mail Project asks visitors to decorate a postcard for someone in town they are grateful for, and the library supplies the card, the art materials and the instructions.

How it works

Participants bring the recipient's address and can stop in at any point between 10 a.m. and 1 p.m. on Saturday, November 14, in the Sydney Wright Room. Registration is required, and the library lists 19 seats. The registration form asks for a name, an email address and a phone number.

Where the cards go

The finished postcards will be displayed at the library and then mailed before Thanksgiving. The recipient can be a person, an agency or a business in Jamestown, which makes the project a small, personal way to recognize a neighbor.

A busy room

The library's calendar this month is full of other programs, including a weekly writing workshop, a knitting group and a Tuesday movie. The Happy Mail Project is listed for adults, and the library's calendar does not name an instructor.
