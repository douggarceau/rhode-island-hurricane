New Tables, New Taps: The Rhode Island Spots Opening Right Now

A Jewelry District bar-and-event house opens in December, Warren gets a walk-in seafood joint, and Bristol's waterfront gets new owners, per Rhode Island Monthly's latest dining roundup.

The Rhode Island restaurant map is redrawing itself again. Rhode Island Monthly's latest Diner's Update, published Nov. 13 by Jamie Coelho, tracks a fresh wave of openings from Providence's Jewelry District to Warren and Bristol, plus a few big names getting makeovers before the new year.

The headliner: Bassett House and The Alibi Room

Two new rooms are "opening this December" at 18 Bassett St. in Providence's Jewelry District, according to the magazine. Bassett House focuses on private events and a seasonal outdoor patio, while The Alibi Room is billed as a "friendly neighborhood bar."

Rhode Island Monthly names Jim Low as a co-owner and Michael Johnson as the chef behind a shared-plates menu. The opening lineup it describes includes lamb chop lollipops, Swedish meatballs, a trio of deviled eggs and Alibi sliders in three styles: chicken cordon bleu, burger or meatball. The venue's website is bassetthousepvd.com.

Open now

La Fe Taqueria has opened on Hope Street in Providence in the former Blue Cottage space, according to the roundup, which says it is partly owned by the owner of Lotus Pepper.

Nitro Bar is "now open at 134 Ives St. in Providence, where Glou used to be," the magazine reports.

Over in Warren, Kingfish is open as a seafood-focused, walk-ins-only spot. Rhode Island Monthly says it runs Thursday through Monday starting at 4 p.m., with "$2 shucks on Mondays."

Coming soon

Chow Fun Food Group is bringing CUT 123, a new concept focused on steaks and burgers, into the former Ten Prime Seafood space, according to the roundup.

In Bristol, the former Quito's at 411 Thames St. has new owners. The magazine says Q's Waterfront by O.M.G. is led by Frank Martucci, Adam "Opie" Oppenheimer and Anthony Guaetta and is planned as a "seafood-forward" waterfront restaurant with a creative beverage program. No opening date has been announced.

Babes Pie Co. is "scheduled to move into the space soon" at 1413 Westminster St. in Providence, the former Sin Bakery storefront, per the update.

In Cranston, Newport Restaurant Group is still developing a new concept at 24 Midway Road in Garden City Center, in the former Applebee's space. The magazine says it will be the group's 19th location and its second at Garden City. The name, chef and opening date have not been announced.

Makeovers on the coast

At Watch Hill in Westerly, Ocean House's COAST has been renovated, and Rhode Island Monthly says the menu now offers three-course, four-course and Chef's Tasting options. The Boat House in Tiverton is closed for renovations and is set to reopen in spring 2027.

Last calls

Not every door is opening. The same roundup reports that Rise 'n' Shine Coffee Bar on Smith Hill closed Nov. 22 and that Sin Bakery closed on Halloween, clearing the way for Babes Pie Co.

Why it matters

The new crop leans hard into the things Rhode Island does best: raw bars, small plates and neighborhood rooms where the bartender learns your name. A Jewelry District bar with a private-event house attached adds another late-night option to a neighborhood that keeps adding residents and offices, and Kingfish's walk-in, oysters-on-Monday model brings a little Narragansett Bay swagger to downtown Warren.

How to go

Kingfish takes walk-ins only, Thursday through Monday from 4 p.m. For Bassett House and The Alibi Room, check bassetthousepvd.com for the December opening date and booking details. Rhode Island Monthly's full Diner's Update has the rest of the month's openings, closings and moves.
