Thanksgiving Weekend in Rhode Island: Buy the Cranberries Today

Big grocery stores close Thursday by state law; then the weekend brings a lighthouse lighting on Goat Island, Warren's festival and Nicky Jam at Amica.

First, the warning: forget the cranberries and you're out of luck. Under Rhode Island law, Thanksgiving is a restricted holiday, and food stores with more than six workers on a shift must stay closed, the Providence Journal has reported. That means Stop & Shop, Market Basket, Trader Joe's, Whole Foods, Aldi and the rest, plus liquor stores. Gas stations, restaurants, pharmacies and small corner stores may open, but call ahead.

Friday

Warren's Holiday Festival fills Friday and Saturday. The Block Island Holiday Stroll runs across the island Friday through Sunday, according to Visit Rhode Island.

Saturday

Newport Harbor Island Resort lights its lighthouse on Goat Island at 5 p.m. Saturday, Nov. 28, with carolers, warm drinks and a view of the Newport Bridge, per Visit Rhode Island. It costs nothing to attend, and parking is validated with a food or drink receipt. The night continues with live music at the resort's 1639 and Torpedo Bar. Same night in Providence: Nicky Jam brings his Perreoland show to Amica Mutual Pavilion.
