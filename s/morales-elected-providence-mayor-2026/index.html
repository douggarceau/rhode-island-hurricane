David Morales, 28, Elected Providence's Youngest Mayor

The state representative and democratic socialist won about 81 percent of the vote over Republican Dave Talan, two months after ousting Mayor Brett Smiley in the primary.

Providence voters on Tuesday elected state Rep. David Morales as the city's next mayor. At 28, he will be the youngest mayor in Providence history.

Morales defeated Republican Dave Talan, a perennial candidate and leader of the Providence Republican Party, winning about 81 percent of the vote. The Associated Press called the race shortly after the polls closed.

A primary upset

The general election was the second step of a fast rise. In September, Morales beat Mayor Brett Smiley in the Democratic primary, ending Smiley's tenure after one term. It was the first time in more than 50 years that a sitting Providence mayor lost a bid for another term; the last time was 1974, when Republican Vincent "Buddy" Cianci beat Democrat Joe Doorley.

Morales centered his campaign on housing costs. He promised to cap rent increases in the city, after Smiley vetoed Providence's first rent stabilization ordinance. He was endorsed by U.S. Sen. Bernie Sanders and by former Providence Mayor Jorge Elorza.

Who he is

Morales grew up in California and came to Providence to attend Brown University. At 20, he was the youngest graduate in the history of Brown's public affairs master's program. He was elected to the Rhode Island House in 2020 at age 22, representing District 7, which covers the Mount Pleasant, Valley and Elmhurst neighborhoods, and he remains the youngest member of the General Assembly. He is a member of the Democratic Socialists of America.

The record he breaks belonged to Joseph Paolino Jr., elected mayor in 1984. Paolino was also 28 when he took office but turned 29 on his second day as mayor.

A new City Hall

For the first time, both Providence's mayor and its City Council president are expected to be democratic socialists. Councilman Miguel Sanchez, also 28, is expected to be chosen council president when the new term begins in January.

Morales will be the city's 40th mayor. He takes office in January.
