Molly Kugler Dickinson's round panels open at Jamestown Arts Center

Friday, Nov. 20, from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. at the Jamestown Arts Center, 18 Valley Street in Jamestown, with the show on view through Jan. 9, 2027.

The Jamestown Arts Center will open Of Longing and Loss, a solo exhibition by painter Molly Kugler Dickinson, with an evening reception on Friday, Nov. 20. The reception runs from 5:30 to 7:30 p.m. at the center's building on Valley Street, and the show stays up until Jan. 9, 2027.

The work

Dickinson's paintings are small and round. She paints on birch panel with acrylic and oil, so the circular shape is part of each piece rather than a frame around it. The featured work is a 16-inch round from this year, titled as a homage to the psychiatrist Viktor Frankl and his book about the search for meaning.

Another panel, Totem #1 (primal places), measures 12 by 18 inches and was also made in 2026. The exhibition page connects her recent paintings to a forest-light series that a judge praised at the center's 2025 members' show.

Gallery hours

Gallery hours are Wednesday through Saturday, 11 a.m. to 3 p.m., and by appointment. The exhibition page does not say whether admission is charged, so check with the center before you visit.

A good evening to go

An opening reception is a relaxed way to see new work. The paintings are intimate in scale, so a little time at each one rewards the visit. The reception runs until 7:30 p.m., giving visitors room to browse at their own pace.
