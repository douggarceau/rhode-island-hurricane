97 State Workers Made $100,000 or More in Overtime Alone

The overtime review in Rhode Island Hurricane's government guide follows every overtime dollar on the state payroll.

In fiscal year 2025, 7,168 people on Rhode Island's state payroll were paid overtime, according to the state's payroll file. Ninety-seven of them made $100,000 or more in overtime alone, and 219 earned more in overtime than in their base pay.

What the overtime review shows

The review breaks overtime down by year since 2011, by agency and by job title. It shows how overtime compares with base pay at each agency, who the top 100 overtime earners were, and who stayed in the top 100 three years running.

Why it matters

Heavy overtime can mean an agency is short-staffed rather than overstaffed, and per hour, time-and-a-half overtime can cost about as much as a new hire with benefits. The review shows the numbers so readers can judge for themselves.
