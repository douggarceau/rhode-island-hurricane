RISD Museum's Barkan Ceramics Show Runs Through March 2028

More than 100 works from the Barkan Contemporary Ceramic Collection are on view now at the RISD Museum in Providence.

Ceramics at the RISD Museum are on view through March 5, 2028, in an exhibition titled A Shared Journey: The Barkan Contemporary Ceramic Collection. The show brings together more than 100 works from a collection that spans the 1960s through the 2010s, and it arrives alongside a major gift to the museum.

A gift to the museum

Fifty-two of the works are being given to the RISD Museum by Hope and Mel Barkan. A featured-exhibition post on the North American Reciprocal Museum Association's website calls the donation the most significant gift of contemporary ceramics in the museum's history.

Two galleries, two chapters

The exhibition opened in September 2025 in the museum's Ceramics Gallery. A second part opened on November 8, 2025, in the Upper Farago Gallery, so visitors who come back in the coming months will see the story continue across two spaces. Elizabeth A. Williams curated the show, which the museum frames around how ceramics foster collaboration and dialogue between makers.

Why it is worth a visit

Because the run extends well into 2028, there is time to see the show more than once. Its long span, from work made in the 1960s to pieces from the 2010s, makes it a rare chance to trace how contemporary ceramics developed over half a century, all in one place in Providence.

Visiting

The museum pages consulted for this story did not list daily hours or admission prices, so check the RISD Museum's website for current hours and admission before you go.
