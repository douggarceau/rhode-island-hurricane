Newport Hospital Regains 'A' Safety Grade in Fall Leapfrog Ratings

The hospital climbed back from a B in the spring, joining Rhode Island Hospital and The Miriam with top marks from the national patient-safety group.

Newport Hospital has earned back an "A" patient-safety grade from The Leapfrog Group in its fall 2026 ratings, six months after slipping to a "B" in the spring round, What's Up Newp reported on Nov. 18.

What happened

The Leapfrog Group, an independent nonprofit, grades general hospitals across the country from A to F twice a year. Newport Hospital had earned an A in every round from 2023 through 2025 before dropping to a B in spring 2026. The fall grade returns it to the top tier, according to What's Up Newp.

Newport Hospital is one of four Brown University Health hospitals to receive an A this fall. The others named in the report are Rhode Island Hospital and The Miriam Hospital in Providence, and Saint Anne's Hospital in Fall River, Massachusetts.

The numbers

Leapfrog's grades are based on more than 30 measures of how well hospitals prevent errors, injuries and infections, along with the systems they have in place to protect patients. The ratings are posted for public use at HospitalSafetyGrade.org.

What's Up Newp listed spring 2026 grades for several other Rhode Island hospitals but did not report their fall grades. In the spring round, three Rhode Island hospitals earned an A.

The fall result means Newport Hospital lost its A for only one rating period. Before the spring dip, it had put together a run of A grades in each of the twice-yearly rounds from 2023 through 2025, the report said. Rhode Island Hospital and The Miriam Hospital, both in Providence, held their A grades from the spring.

Who said what

Dr. Tenny Thomas, Newport Hospital's president and chief medical officer, described the A as a tremendous source of pride for the hospital's staff and credited its culture of quality and safety, according to the report.

Brown University Health also noted that Saint Anne's Hospital has now earned 30 consecutive A grades, which the health system said puts it among only 11 hospitals nationwide with that record.

What it means for residents

Newport Hospital is the main acute-care hospital for Aquidneck Island and nearby communities. Safety grades are one of several tools patients can use when comparing hospitals for planned procedures, alongside advice from their own doctors, insurance coverage and distance from home. Leapfrog's ratings focus specifically on patient safety rather than on every aspect of care.

The grades apply to general hospitals, and each one is assigned on the same A-to-F scale so hospitals can be compared side by side. Because the ratings are refreshed twice a year, a single grade reflects a hospital's performance over a recent window of time rather than a permanent ranking.

What comes next

Leapfrog updates its grades every six months, so the next round of ratings is expected in the spring of 2027. Patients can look up any hospital's current and past grades on the HospitalSafetyGrade.org website.
