John Crist Live Sunday Night at The VETS in Providence

Sunday, Nov. 15 at 7 p.m. at The VETS in Providence, comedian John Crist brings his stand-up and viral sketch comedy tour, with doors at 6 p.m.

Comedian John Crist brings his John Crist Live! tour to The VETS, the Veterans Memorial Auditorium in downtown Providence, on Sunday, November 15. The show starts at 7 p.m., and doors open at 6 p.m., so there is time to find seats and settle in before the lights go down.

The tour mixes stand-up comedy with viral sketch comedy, a combination that suits an audience looking for a shared laugh on a Sunday evening. It is a good choice for a group of friends or a couple who want something light and social before the holiday season takes over.

Tickets are on sale now through The VETS website, at the box office, and by phone at (401) 421-2787. The listing we checked did not show prices, so check the box office for seat options and current fees before you go.

The VETS is at 1 Avenue of the Arts in Providence. The same hall hosts the Ballet RI Nutcracker in December, with performances from December 17 through 27, so the building has a busy stretch ahead.
