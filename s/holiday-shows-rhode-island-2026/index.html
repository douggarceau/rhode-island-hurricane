Rhode Island's Holiday Shows: Scrooge's 50th, Cirque du Soleil and Disney On Ice

Trinity Rep celebrates 50 years of A Christmas Carol, Cirque makes its PPAC debut, and the Boston Pops come to town.

The holiday season starts early on Rhode Island stages this year, and there's something for every age. Here's the lineup, in order.

A Christmas Carol at Trinity Rep: Nov. 5–Dec. 31

Trinity Rep's beloved staging turns 50 this year. The company is billing it as "Celebrating 50 Years of Holiday Cheer," with original music by Richard Cumming. This year's director, resident company member Taavon Gamble, has appeared in eight productions of the show himself. Performances are in the Dowling Theater in downtown Providence.

'Twas the Night Before... by Cirque du Soleil: Nov. 24–Dec. 6

It's Cirque du Soleil's first-ever engagement at PPAC, and the company's first holiday show made for families. A girl named Isabella feels she's outgrown her family's tradition of reading "A Visit from Saint Nicholas," until the poem comes to life with aerial straps, hoop diving and hair suspension set to reimagined Christmas favorites. There are 19 performances. On BankNewport Family Night, Dec. 2 at 7 p.m., each regularly priced ticket comes with a ticket for a child 18 or under at no charge.

Boston Pops Holiday Concert: Dec. 13

The Boston Pops bring their holiday concert to PPAC on Sunday, Dec. 13 at 7 p.m. The same day, the Rhode Island Philharmonic performs Handel's Messiah.

Alicia Keys' Hell's Kitchen: Dec. 15–20

The Broadway musical built on Alicia Keys' songs closes out PPAC's year.

Disney On Ice: Dec. 23–28

Disney On Ice: Magic in the Stars skates into the Amica Mutual Pavilion over Christmas week, with shows Dec. 23, Dec. 24 at noon, and Dec. 26–28.

Tickets

Holiday shows sell out. PPAC's box office is at 401-421-ARTS (2787), and Trinity Rep tickets are at trinityrep.com. All of these dates are in the Hurricane calendar.
