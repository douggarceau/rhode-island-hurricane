Young performers take the mic at Youth On the Mic in Westerly

Sunday, Nov. 22 at 5 p.m. at The Knick Music Lab, 35 Railroad Avenue in Westerly, with no charge for admission.

Youth On the Mic is an open mic series for students in sixth through twelfth grade who live in Rhode Island or southeastern Connecticut. The next show is Sunday, Nov. 22, at 5 p.m. at The Knick Music Lab, 35 Railroad Avenue in Westerly. Admission is no charge.

Performers can sing, play an instrument or take the stage in another way. They can bring original material or cover songs they love. The United Theatre's listing describes the series as now in its seventh year and as running bi-monthly.

A supportive room

The organizers describe the evening as a low-pressure alternative to adult open mics, a place where young performers can play for a live audience. The listing also notes that concessions are not available at these events, so plan to eat beforehand.

Before you go

The listing names a single date, Sunday, Nov. 22. For families and friends, the appeal is seeing young local talent up close in a small room, with each performer getting a turn in front of a live audience.

The series is built around a simple idea: give students a real audience and a short turn at a microphone, in a setting designed to feel less daunting than an adult open mic. Check the United Theatre's show page for any change before you leave home.
