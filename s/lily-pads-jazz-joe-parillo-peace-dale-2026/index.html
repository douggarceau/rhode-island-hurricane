Joe Parillo and the New Ensemble Bring Lily Pads Jazz to Peace Dale

Sunday, Nov. 15, doors at 3 p.m. and the show from 3:30 to 5:30 p.m., at the Unitarian Universalist Congregation of South County in Peace Dale.

Jazz comes to South County on Sunday afternoon when Joe Parillo and the New Ensemble perform as part of the Lily Pads Sunday Jazz Series. Doors open at 3 p.m., and the show runs from 3:30 to 5:30 p.m. Tickets are $25.

Parillo is a pianist, composer and educator. He leads a group that includes Wendy Klein on flute, George Dussault on guitar, Paula Clare on vocals, Rick Andre on drums, Lou Bocciarelli on bass and Rick Couto on percussion. The concert was rescheduled from September 25.

About the series

The Unitarian Universalist Congregation of South County launched the series to bring live jazz to South County. Proceeds are split equally between the musicians and the congregation, which uses its share for local artists and its community programs. The organizers hope the series becomes a regular gathering place for musicians and listeners, and that it may welcome other kinds of music in time.

Tickets and where to go

Tickets are $25 through the series' online payment page, and they are also sold at the door. The concert is listed at the congregation at 27 North Road in Peace Dale, but the series' announcement did not spell out the November venue, so confirm the location with the congregation before you head out.
