Westerly Library's December: Stained Glass, Gingerbread and a Talk

Wednesday, Dec. 2, at 5 p.m. in Westerly, with an art exhibition in the Hoxie Gallery and a gingerbread walk-through in the Old Main Reading Room.

The Westerly Library's public calendar for the next three weeks is full of the quiet, hands-on programs that make a library feel like a community room. A few items stand out for anyone looking for something to do with friends or family.

Gingerbread and a new exhibition on Dec. 2

The busiest evening is Wednesday, Dec. 2. Several events are listed for 5 p.m. The Gingerbread House Competition takes place in the Old Main Reading Room. Visitors walk through the gingerbread houses and vote, and the listing gives the event three hours and says all ages are welcome.

The same hour, the library lists its December exhibition in the Hoxie Gallery. The show, titled ex Libris and credited to Eddie Hall, features reclaimed stained-glass windows painted with abstract designs. Also at 5 p.m. that evening, a Crafternoon To-Go session called Handmade Holiday takes place at The Pop-Up at Coy, offering a holiday craft kit or activity for families, adults, teens and older adults.

A film and an author talk

On Thursday, Dec. 3, at 6 p.m., the auditorium hosts a Green Planet Films screening of The New Peasants. The listing describes it as a film for families, adults and older adults, and gives a running time of about an hour and a half.

On Saturday, Dec. 5, at 2:30 p.m., a one-hour author talk takes place in the auditorium. The speaker will discuss writing the novel Alma of Rag Town.

Weekly stitching and needlework

Two regular groups meet at the library for people who work with their hands. Sewers in Stitches meets every Monday from 2 to 4 p.m. in the Terrace Room. It is a sewing and quilting group for adults and older adults at every skill level. Members share ideas and advice, and attendees bring their own supplies. The Westerly Area Needleworkers meet on Saturday, Nov. 21, and again on Saturday, Dec. 5, at 9 a.m. in the library.

Before you go

The library's listings do not state admission costs for these events, so call the library before you go. The calendar covers the period from Nov. 15 through Dec. 6.
