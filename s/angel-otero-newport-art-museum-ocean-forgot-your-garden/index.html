Angel Otero's Layered Atlantic at the Newport Art Museum

"The Ocean Forgot Your Garden" is on view through Jan. 10, 2027, at 76 Bellevue Avenue, Newport.

The Newport Art Museum is hosting a major exhibition of the painter Angel Otero, Angel Otero: The Ocean Forgot Your Garden, on view through January 10, 2027. It takes the Atlantic as its subject, as a force of labor, migration and memory connecting Puerto Rico and Newport.

How the paintings are made

Otero was born in 1981 in Santurce, Puerto Rico, and works between Puerto Rico and New York. His method is unusual: he builds layers of oil paint on glass, peels them off as thin skins, and transfers them to canvas, so that earlier gestures remain visible beneath later ones. The surfaces read as depth and weather, an apt match for an exhibition about water.

Domestic objects, instruments and furniture drift through the ocean-like spaces of the canvases, many of them drawn from the artist's childhood. The effect is of a household carried across the sea, or remembered through it.

Beyond the main galleries

The exhibition extends into the museum's Griswold House, where eight paintings from Otero's Poussin series are on view. The series reimagines works by the seventeenth-century painter Nicolas Poussin, tying the Newport show to a long tradition of European painting.

Otero's work is held in collections including the Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum and the Whitney Museum of American Art, and he received the Leonore Annenberg Fellowship in the Visual Arts in 2010.

Visiting

The museum is at 76 Bellevue Avenue, Newport, RI 02840 and is open Monday and Wednesday through Sunday, 11 a.m. to 4 p.m.; it is closed Tuesdays. Adult admission is $20, seniors 65 and over $15, college students $10. Members, active military and their spouses, and visitors 17 and under are admitted at no charge. The museum can be reached at (401) 848-8200.

With two months of fall and winter remaining, it is a rewarding stop for anyone looking for a serious painting show close to home.
