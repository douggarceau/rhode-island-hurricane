Stargazing Fridays at Frosty Drew Observatory in Charlestown

Every Friday, weather permitting, from 7:00 to 10:00 PM at 62 Park Lane in Ninigret Park, Charlestown; adults $5 and children under 5 at no charge.

On clear Friday evenings, the Frosty Drew Observatory and Science Center in Charlestown opens for Stargazing Nights, a chance to look up through real telescopes with astronomers on hand. The program runs from 7:00 to 10:00 PM, weather permitting, and it sometimes continues later, until the skies can no longer be observed or the last visitor heads home.

Visitors can spend time in the observatory itself, in the Sky Theatre, and at the telescopes. The astronomers aim the instruments at the night's targets, which the organizers list as Saturn's rings, the craters of the Moon, Jupiter's four large moons, the polar ice caps of Mars, the phases of Venus, bright and massive stars, star clusters, nebulae and distant galaxies. The observatory also promotes what it calls the best Milky Way views in Rhode Island, so a clear, dark evening is the best time to come.

Admission is $5 for adults, and children under 5 get in at no charge. The observatory sits at 62 Park Lane in Ninigret Park, Charlestown, RI 02813. Because the program runs every Friday, a family can pick the clear night that fits its calendar, and a red-light flashlight helps keep night vision sharp once the lights go down.

For questions or the current schedule, call (401) 859-1450, email astronomer@frostydrew.org, or visit frostydrew.org/events.
