Gallery Night Providence Throws Open Its Doors on Nov. 19

Thursday, Nov. 19 from 5 to 8 p.m., with galleries across Providence hosting openings and visits, starting with the RISD Museum at 20 North Main Street.

Gallery Night Providence returns on Thursday, November 19, when galleries across the city open their doors from 5 to 8 p.m. for openings and visits. The RISD Museum's listing describes the evening as a citywide night of gallery openings and visits, and the museum at 20 North Main Street is one of the starting points.

Gallery Night is held on the third Thursday of the month, a regular rhythm that gives Providence's art community a predictable reason to get out and look at new work. A RISD Museum page for an earlier Gallery Night described no-charge guided tours of galleries across the city, including the museum, so visitors who want company on the circuit should check the current listings for tour details.

Because the galleries are spread around the city, the easiest way to take in the evening is to pick two or three stops in advance rather than trying to see everything. An earlier listing noted that the museum closes earlier than some other stops on the circuit, so plan the museum visit first if it is on your list.

Gallery openings are also a good chance to talk with artists and gallery staff about the work on the walls, which is a large part of the appeal of the evening.

Providence's art scene spans museums, college galleries and independent spaces, so the evening can be as simple as a walk between two or three storefronts or as full as a planned route. Pairing a stop at the RISD Museum with a visit to a smaller gallery gives a good sense of how varied the city's art community is.
