Songs of Gratitude Mark St. Mary of the Bay's 175th Anniversary

Sunday, Nov. 8 at 3 p.m. at St. Mary of the Bay, 645 Main Street, Warren, with Michael DiMucci in voice and piano.

St. Mary of the Bay in Warren marks its 175th anniversary this Sunday with THANKFUL: Songs of Gratitude, a concert at 3 p.m. in the church at 645 Main Street. Michael DiMucci performs a program of songs about gratitude, singing and playing the piano.

An anniversary concert is a natural way for a parish to say thank you. A 175-year history means generations of parishioners and neighbors have kept the church's life going, and a program built around gratitude gives the afternoon a clear, warm theme for the congregation and visitors alike.

The format is simple: one voice and one piano in a church setting. That lets the songs carry the afternoon. It is also a fitting way to begin the week of Thanksgiving, which falls later this month, when many families are already thinking about the things they are thankful for.

Warren sits on the water of the East Bay, and a Sunday afternoon in early November is a pleasant time to walk the town's Main Street before or after the concert.

Details

Date: Sunday, Nov. 8. Time: 3 p.m. Place: St. Mary of the Bay, 645 Main Street, Warren, RI. The parish calendar does not list admission, so contact the parish or check the diocese's events page before you go.
