Jay Leno Brings His Stand-Up to the Stadium Theatre Nov. 6

The former Tonight Show host plays Woonsocket's Monument Square on Friday, Nov. 6 at 8 p.m., with the Jack Babineau Band opening.

Late-night TV legend Jay Leno is coming to Woonsocket. He plays the Stadium Theatre, 28 Monument Square, on Friday, Nov. 6 at 8 p.m.

The hardest-working man in show business

That's how the Stadium Theatre bills him, and the numbers back it up: Leno still does more than 100 stand-up shows a year, on top of hosting Jay Leno's Garage on CNBC and the syndicated You Bet Your Life. He's also a best-selling children's book author. The Jack Babineau Band opens the night.

Tickets

Tickets are $86, $96, $111 and $136 through the Stadium Theatre at stadiumtheatre.com. The theater warns that it can't help with tickets bought from third-party resale sites, so buy direct.
