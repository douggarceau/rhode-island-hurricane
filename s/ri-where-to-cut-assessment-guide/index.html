Where to Cut: Our Assessment of Nine Areas of Rhode Island Government

Rhode Island Hurricane's view of where the state could shrink or restructure, and the strongest argument against each.

Rhode Island Hurricane has reviewed the state payroll and budget for signs of where government could shrink or be restructured. The assessment is our opinion, built only on public numbers, and each verdict includes the strongest argument against it.

The nine verdicts

Statewide management layers: cut candidate. Executive Office of Health and Human Services: trim and restructure. Department of Administration: trim and restructure. Department of Corrections: fix overtime, not headcount. Behavioral Healthcare, Developmental Disabilities and Hospitals: fix overtime, not headcount. Department of Business Regulation: review. Small commissions and offices: trim and restructure. Department of Transportation: review. Public Safety, including the State Police: hold.
