Newport Art Museum Hosts a Collage Workshop and a Printmaking Demo

Saturday, Nov. 21, from noon to 3 p.m. and Sunday, Nov. 22, from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the Newport Art Museum in Newport.

Newport Art Museum has two hands-on programs on its calendar this weekend, and both are built for people who want to make something rather than only look at it. One is a writing and collage workshop on Saturday. The other is a printmaking lecture and demonstration on Sunday morning.

Writing and collage on Saturday

The first event is called Fragments of Art and Memory and is listed as a writing and collage workshop. It runs Saturday, Nov. 21, from noon to 3 p.m. Collage means building an image from cut and layered pieces, and the program pairs that making with writing.

Printmaking on Sunday

The second event is titled Printmaking Lecture and Demonstration with Bill Jacklin. The museum's calendar places it from 10 a.m. to 1 p.m. on Sunday, Nov. 22. The title promises both a talk and a live demonstration of printmaking. The calendar tags it as a workshop, so anyone hoping to pull a print of their own should confirm that before arriving.

Before you go

The calendar listings for these two programs do not give prices, so check the museum's events page or call the museum before you go. The same stretch of the calendar also lists an abstract-art program on Nov. 18 and a Gauguin program on Nov. 19, both covered elsewhere on this site.
